Version: 2.0.1
Date: 2026-10-14
  Features:
    - Third radar mode "Geological survey" (with RSO Reforged): each scan marks on the map the nearest
      field the force has not charted yet (generated or not), with its resource and rough size, and
      announces it in chat; falls back to ore when nothing is left around. With RSO Reforged 9.2.0 the mark
      also shows the grade of the field and its rich vein, infinite core or guards. Kept by copy-paste, cloning and blueprints.
    - Every scan reveals either an ore chunk or an unusual signal: an undiscovered ruin of the
      Paranoidal ruin journal marked with "?" on the map and announced in chat.
    - Clicking the radar opens a window that selects the mode of each radar: "Resources" (default) reveals
      only ore chunks, "Unusual signals" marks ruins and reveals ore when no ruin is left. The mode is
      kept by copy-paste, cloning and blueprints.
  Changes:
    - A scan tries up to eight random ore chunks and prefers chunks the force has not charted yet.
    - The ore index is rebuilt only when Ore Radar itself changes, not on every configuration change.
    - The item is sorted after the standard radars.
  Bugfixes:
    - A scan is no longer wasted when the randomly chosen ore chunk is depleted.
    - Declares the optional dependency on Bob's Electronics used by the recipe.
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
    - Factorio 2.1 radars have their own window, which hid the mode window opened on a click: the mode
      choice is now a panel on the right of the ore radar's window, and the click control ore-radar-open is removed.

Version: 2.0.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - Ports the geological radar from Paranoidal 1.1 to Factorio 2.0, retaining its graphics, recipe and research.
  Changes:
    - Indexes existing and newly generated chunks in a bounded background queue.
    - Shares a budget of eight work items and at most four resource charts per tick across all radars.
    - Disables the tick handler while idle and reduces nearby map refresh to the vanilla cadence.
  Bugfixes:
    - Removes depleted ore chunks from the correct surface index in constant time.
    - Handles surface renames, clearing, deletion and save/load.
    - Preserves completed scans while indexes are rebuilt and across configuration changes.
