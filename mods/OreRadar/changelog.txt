Version: 2.0.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - Ports the geological radar from Paranoidal 1.1 to Factorio 2.0, retaining its graphics, recipe and research.
  Changes:
    - Indexes existing and newly generated chunks in a bounded background queue.
    - Shares a budget of eight work items and at most four resource charts per tick across all radars.
    - Disables the tick handler while idle and reduces nearby map refresh to the vanilla cadence.
  Bugfixes:
    - Removes depleted ore chunks from the correct surface index in constant time.
    - Handles surface renames, clearing, deletion and save/load.
    - Preserves completed scans while indexes are rebuilt and across configuration changes.
