Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-10-31
  Major Features:
    - Paranoidal fork of Pipe Visualizer 2.0 2.4.4 under a new name; the runtime is rewritten for speed. Key bindings, shortcuts, setting names and the PipeVisualizer remote interface are kept.
    - The drawing follows building, removal, rotation and flipping of fluid entities: new entities join a shown network or the overlay, removed ones disappear, neighbours update their connections.
  Features:
    - Setting "Show on the map" (per player, on by default): without it nothing is drawn in chart mode, half the render objects.
    - Mouse-over keeps the shown systems while the cursor is over their entities or over entities without fluid; they are removed on empty ground or on another fluid system, not on every selection change.
    - Pumps and other entities whose fluidbox has no fluid segment of its own show arrows for the networks they connect, and Y on them shows those networks.
    - Toggling colour by fluid/system recolours the shown systems as well, not only the overlay.
  Optimizations:
    - Underground connections are two dashed lines per pair (world and map) instead of two sprites per tile.
    - Every render object is created once with its final sprite and colour; the hidden object pool is removed.
    - The overlay scans chunk by chunk within the per-tick budget, nearest chunks first; opening it is no longer one long tick. Chunks that leave the view are removed over the next ticks.
    - The overlay searches only entities with fluidboxes or a fluid energy source (names from prototypes) instead of all inserters, labs and loaders.
    - Removing a system touches only its own entities; settings are read once per player instead of per entity.
    - The save keeps only render objects, system ids and chunk lists, no fluidbox or pipe connection data.
    - on_tick, selection, position and building events are subscribed only while they are needed.
    - Fluid colours and order are computed from prototypes in every Lua state instead of being stored.
  Bugfixes:
    - Removing one system (mouse-over leaving, Y on a shown system) no longer erases every other shown system.
    - Colour by fluid uses the contents of the whole fluid segment, not the fluidbox the walk started from.
    - Y and mouse-over on an already shown system no longer walk it again.
    - Entities still queued when their system was removed are no longer drawn as grey boxes.
    - Hovering a part of a network that is still being drawn no longer breaks the drawing.
    - The overlay no longer turns into a network walk beyond the screen after Y or mouse-over, and no longer loses its background when the last mouse-over system is removed.
    - The overlay follows the player to another surface (Factorissimo buildings, remote view); render objects are no longer lost between surfaces.
    - Valves are shown in the overlay.
    - Input arrows point into the entity; they were turned by a quarter turn on two sides.
    - "Ultrawide support" doubles the overlay range instead of replacing the range scale; the scale is limited to 8.
    - Colours by system no longer repeat the first hue and cycle after 72 systems instead of turning grey.
    - Pumps are coloured by their own networks, not all by the fluid of the first pump.
    - A failed entity no longer cancels the rest of the tick's drawing.
    - The remote interface is available from other mods' on_init, and its lists survive configuration changes.
    - Overlay settings apply at once; data of removed players and deleted surfaces is cleared.
  Graphics:
    - pv-fluid-arrow and pv-fluid-arrow-input-output use the pixel-identical files of the core mod; the underground connection sprite is no longer needed.
    - PNG files recompressed with pingo -s4 -quality=45: the graphics stay pixel-identical (19.0 -> 6.2 KB), thumbnail.png is lossy at 35.1 dB PSNR (20.4 -> 11.1 KB).
  Changes:
    - flib is no longer a dependency.
