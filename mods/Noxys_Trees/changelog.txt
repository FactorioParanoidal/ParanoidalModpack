Version: 9.5.3
  Changes:
    - Version number starts with 9 (Paranoidal fork): the Mod Portal never offers an "update" over local changes.
    - Paranoidal local maintenance of 0.5.2 (not an upstream release).
    - Service runtime work every 30 ticks, retaining operation counts and fractional scheduling phase.
    - Pause the service while disabled and restore it on load or setting changes.
    - Combine proximity queries, reuse player-force lists within a chunk and remove the unused stored list.
    - Restrict the one-time planted-tree exclusion scan to tree prototypes.
    - Keep the missing-uranium guard, original random draws and gameplay settings.
    - Restore the 1.1 spreading exclusions for Angel's trees, gardens and puffer nests under their 2.0 names.
    - Never kill the growing trees of Bio Industries (bio-tree-<tree>-<stage>), for any reason: Bio Industries decides their survival by the soil, while Noxys killed them next to the player's own seedlings and buildings. Grown trees are treated as before.
  Bug Fixes:
    - Growing trees of Bio Industries (bio-tree-<tree>-<stage>) no longer spread: the copies were not in its grow tables, so they never grew up and gave seedlings when cut.
