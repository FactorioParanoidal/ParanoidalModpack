Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026.10.25
  Info:
    - Renamed to robotarmy-reforged (title Robot Army Reforged); incompatible with the original robotarmy mod (same prototype names).
    - Paranoidal fork. The control script is rewritten; save compatibility with 2.4.x is not kept (a save with 2.4.x data starts the mod state over and picks up the droids and buildings found on the map).
  Balancing:
    - The droid technology tree starts in the chemical science era and uses military science packs throughout: Army robotics (after chemical science pack, military 3, automation 3, circuit network) -> Riflebot -> Battle droid -> Rocket droid / Flamebot -> Terminator (with utility science, after military 4, power armour and laser turrets 2); Army robotics -> Defender -> Distractor -> Destroyer robot units (after the matching Bob's robot technologies). A technology also asks for the science packs of its prerequisites from other mods.
    - Recipes are rebuilt from Bob's, Angels and Paranoidal parts and cost about five times more: weapon, frame or armour (invar, cobalt steel, titanium, modular or power armour), actuators (bearings, gears, electric engines), power and control (circuits, batteries, processing units). Flying units are built from ten Bob's combat robots of their type and five combat brains of the same tier. Without these mods every ingredient falls back to a vanilla item.
    - The droid assembler and the guard station are built from an assembling machine 3 and intermediate structure components.
    - Droid recipes take about twice as long to craft.
    - Droid health and damage are raised for the new prices: Riflebot 100 health, 20 physical every 1.5 s; Battle droid 250, 12 physical; Rocket droid 200, its rocket hits every enemy within 2 tiles (275 explosion, not the droids); Flamebot 400; Terminator 800; Scout 150, 8 physical; Repair droid 250, repairs 15 health per second; Heavy assault droid 900, 14 physical; Chemical droid 200; Sniper 50 health, range 28.
    - Flying units have half the health and damage of the ten combat robots they are built from (as five vanilla robots, measured on the test stand): Defender unit 300 health, 120 damage per second; Distractor unit 450, 37.5; Destroyer unit 300, 300.
    - Droid weapons have their own ammo categories (droid bullets, rockets, energy weapons, flamethrowers, electric weapons) and research branches after the droid that brings them, levels 4+ with utility science: bullet damage 1-5 (+20 %) and rate of fire 1-4 (+10 %), rocket and energy weapon damage 1-4 (+20 %) and rate of fire 1-3 (+15 %), flamethrower and electric weapon rate of fire 1-3 (+15 %). Measured on the test stand: a damage bonus changes bullet, rocket and laser projectile attacks of units, not flamethrower fire and beams; a rate of fire bonus changes all of them. The vanilla bullet, rocket and laser research no longer affects droids.
  Features:
    - New droids (models of the existing droids with a colour tint unless noted; recipes from Bob's/Angels/Paranoidal parts, vanilla fallbacks; chemical + military science): Scout Droid (after Riflebot, Bob's radar 2: fast, sees far and charts the map two chunks around it), Sniper Droid (after Riflebot, Schall sniper rifle: range 28, 90 damage every 2.5 s, fragile; Yuoki CB4 walker model), Miner Droid (after Riflebot and land mines: rifle, its deploy recipe loads 10 land mines, in battle it lays one every 5 s, not next to another mine), Repair Droid (after Battle Droid, Bob's repair pack 3: repairs its squad, 15 health per second per repair droid shared among the damaged members), Laser Rifleman (after Battle Droid and Bob's laser rifle: 20 laser every 0.5 s, range 18; Yuoki ROBO1 walker model), Tesla Droid (after Laser Rifleman and Bob's battery 2: chain lightning, 25 electric per hit and up to four jumps to enemies around the target), Chemical Droid (after Rocket Droid: throws poison capsules 8-24 tiles; the cloud only hurts what breathes, not droids), Heavy Assault Droid (after Flamebot: armoured, rapid heavy machine gun, slow), Plasma Terminator (after Terminator and Bob's plasma turret 1: built from a terminator, plasma bolts of 150 Bob's plasma damage that also hit enemies within 1.5 tiles, 1200 health).
    - Squads resting at a droid assembler (retreated or recalled) and garrisons at their guard station are repaired; map setting "Repair at assemblers and guard stations", percent of max health per second (default 2, 0 = off).
    - Droid item and entity tooltips (and Factoriopedia) show health, attack (damage, type, interval), range, speed and the special ability.
    - The droid assembler and the guard station connect to the circuit network (enable/disable by condition, read contents, set the deploy recipe).
    - Squad command tool: Shift + right-drag merges the squads of the selected droids into the biggest one (garrisons are not merged) and picks it.
    - Army window: squad health bar in every row; "Recall all" / "Release all" buttons (garrisons are not touched).
    - Droid assembler panel: the squad gathering at the assembler and how many droids it still needs to hunt.
    - The picked squad's label and the line to its destination are also drawn on the map.
    - Ranks (the HeroTurrets principle, with its ten rank names as Paranoidal names them for turrets: Lance Corporal, Corporal, Sergeant, Sergeant Major, Lieutenant, Captain, Major, Lieutenant Colonel, Colonel, General): every droid counts its kills; the kills for each rank are the default HeroTurrets reference values x 0.2 spread linearly, 10 / 120 / 230 / ... / 1000, times a factor of the droid kind (x1.5 for droids that hit many enemies at once: rocket, tesla, plasma terminator, flamebot, Bob's flamethrower drone; x1.25 for rapid fire: heavy assault droid, terminator, defender and destroyer units, Bob's gun and laser drones; x0.5 for support droids: repair, scout, miner, chemical). A promoted droid is replaced by the rank prototype of its kind in the same place and squad, with its quality, health share, kills and land mines. Every kind has its own growth per rank that adds up with every rank (health, damage, range, speed, rate of fire up to 4x, regeneration, resistances up to 95 %, repair power). Over a rank droid, centred: the insignia of its rank from HeroTurrets (used from that mod when it is active; without it a star and the rank number); its icon has a star and the rank number. Tooltips show the rank and the kills for the next rank; pointing at a droid shows its rank, kills and kills to the next rank; the army window shows the kills of every squad and its veterans.
    - Veterans cannot be mined or picked up as items (the rank would be lost): the command tool skips them; only a drop pod carries them.
    - Drop pod (after Battle Droid): "Load" in the army window puts up to 32 droids of a squad (veterans first) with rank, kills, quality, health and land mines into an empty drop pod in the player's cursor or inventory; the squad must be within 30 tiles, garrisons cannot be loaded; the item tooltip lists the squad. Placed on the ground the pod opens after 3 s and the droids land as one squad that holds the landing site until it is released or sent with the tool; the empty pod can be mined back. A pod destroyed before it opens loses its squad; a pod with a squad cannot be mined or blueprinted.
    - Patrol the base (button of a squad in the army window): the squad walks from chunk to neighbouring chunk with buildings of its force (any building, rails, poles, pipes, belts and walls included), least visited first, fights the enemies it meets and never crosses chunks without buildings, so it does not walk to colonies that are not connected by buildings. The base is found from the patrolling squads outwards and kept up to date by checking one chunk every 13 ticks (round robin over the bases with a patrol). After an alarm the squad goes back to patrolling.
    - Rally point of a droid assembler (buttons in its panel, then a click with the command tool, at most 50 tiles away): squads gather, merge and are repaired there instead of next to the output side; shown in Alt mode; copy-paste between assemblers copies it at the same offset.
    - Bob's gun, laser and flamethrower drones join the army after "Bob's drones in the army" (after Army robotics and Bob's drone research): deploy recipes for the droid assembler, drones placed by hand join squads, and they get ranks too.
    - The droid counter also outputs the squads hunting, fighting, gathering, recalled and patrolling, the droids waiting to deploy and an alarm signal (1 while squads respond to a destroyed building); a panel next to its window explains every signal and shows its value.
  Optimizations:
    - The squad integrity check no longer reads the unit group member list once per member (was O(n^2) API calls per check); membership is read from each droid's parent group.
    - script.active_mods is no longer read every tick for every force and on every squad update (each read built a table of all active mods).
    - on_entity_died uses event filters (force and unit types) instead of handling every death in the game; QRF settings are cached.
    - Built events use name filters; destroyed droids and buildings are removed through on_object_destroyed instead of growing tables (storage.units was never cleaned before).
    - Droid assemblers and guard stations are checked round robin, evenly spread over the ticks; one count_entities_filtered call with all droid names instead of eight.
    - Droid counters are written only when a count changes, with one assignment of the section filters (each set_slot/clear_slot raised an event for every listening mod every second); only forces that use the mod are processed.
    - No per-tick work for forces without droids or buildings; retreat merging runs from the squad update instead of scanning every assembler every tick.
    - Hunting squads that find no enemy search less often (up to every 30 s) until they find one.
    - Proximity searches use the cached squad position before reading the unit group position.
    - The always-on logger (an endlessly growing script-output/logs/robotarmy file) and the session statistics are removed; an optional debug setting writes to factorio-current.log.
  Bugfixes:
    - Adding the mod to a save no longer runs force.reset_recipes()/reset_technologies() for every force (it disabled recipes unlocked by scripts of other mods); the old migrations are removed.
    - Deployed droids keep the quality of the droid item, and quality modules in the droid assembler can no longer raise it (deploy recipes have no product); picked up droids return as items of their quality and health; refused duplicate buildings are returned with their quality.
    - Guard stations spawn on their own surface (surface 1 was hard-coded).
    - QRF ignores squads on other surfaces, does not overwrite the saved state of a squad that is already responding, and does not command squads while Unit Control is active.
    - Retreating squads only consider assemblers on their own surface.
    - Spawn positions are searched for the droid's collision box instead of the building's.
    - Guard garrisons get an order to stay at their station and are sent back when they wander off more than 15 tiles; failure handling keeps them in guard mode.
    - Droids created by other mods (script_raised_built/revive, cloning) join squads.
    - Selection and pick-up use the surface of the selection (map view on another surface).
    - The tool hotkey works (the old hotkeys had action = "lua" without a handler).
    - A droid kicked out of its squad is no longer left without a squad when there is no assembler.
    - Counters and guard stations are removed from the tables when destroyed.
    - Land mines of the force that go off no longer call the quick reaction force.
    - The droid damage multiplier also applies to the distractor unit laser and to the flamebot fire stream (both used vanilla damage unscaled).
  Changes:
    - Deploying without placeholder items: a deploy recipe consumes the droid item in a droid assembler or guard station and every finished craft becomes a droid. When there is no room (too many droids around the assembler, full garrison) the finished droids wait and the machine pauses; a removed machine drops its waiting droids as items. The eight "deployable" items are removed.
    - One squad command tool instead of two: drag over a squad to pick it, Shift + drag to send it (attack-move), right-drag to pick droids up. One shortcut and one hotkey.
    - Army window (shortcut "Droid army" or its hotkey): squad thresholds of the force (hunt from, retreat at, hunt radius) with sliders, and the list of squads with composition, state and actions: show on the map, pick, recall or release.
    - Recalled squads go back to the nearest droid assembler and wait there until they are released or sent with the tool.
    - Panel next to the droid assembler and guard station window: droids waiting to be deployed; the guard station sets its own garrison size (copy-paste between stations copies it).
    - The droid settings module and its four signals are removed; their settings are in the army window and the guard station panel.
    - A picked squad shows only to the picking player: a mark on every member, a label with the squad number and size, and a line to where it is going (instead of a slowing sticker entity).
    - Alerts with a map marker instead of chat messages for responding squads (removed when the last one is back), squads without an assembler and stuck squads; the lost squad message has a map link. Flying text instead of chat for the tool.
    - Hunting squads that become too small retreat on their next update; finished orders of a squad are followed by a new order on its next update.
    - Droid assembler: 3 module slots (the 1.1 module_specification was ignored by 2.0, so it had none).
    - Map settings: QRF for vehicles, QRF for walls, QRF alert, targeting mode (nearest / defend assembler / keep radius clear; the last two were only reachable by editing the code), teleporting stray droids, squad alerts, debug log.
    - Startup settings: army loot chest (off; the chest, its recipe and unlock exist only when on), harmless acid puddles (off; the original always removed the damage of every acid splash fire), droid health and damage multipliers.
    - Remote interface "robotarmy-reforged": squads, thresholds, set_threshold, spawner, set_garrison, tracked_units, squad_of, droid, set_patrol, territory, set_rally, pod_load, pod_place.
    - The guard station takes two ingredients (the miner deploy recipe also takes its land mines).
    - Descriptions no longer promise that the sniper and the rocket droid outrange worms.
    - Storage schema 3: a save with older data of this mod starts the mod state over (droids and buildings on the map are picked up again, their ranks with them; kill counters start at 0).
    - The droid counter outputs every droid type as the item signal of its droid (all ranks of a kind together, flying units and Bob's drones included) plus "Droids alive"; the five per-type virtual signals and their icons are removed.
  Graphics:
    - Droid counter activity LED uses the 2.0 sprite size; the loot chest has a 2.0 circuit connector.
    - Symmetric selection boxes of the ground droids.
    - Deploy recipes show the droid icon.
    - Item, unit and technology icons of the new droids are tinted copies of the existing ones; their models are the existing droid sprites with a tint on the body layer (shadows untinted). With Yuoki Industries active, the Sniper Droid uses the animated CB4 walker and the Laser Rifleman the ROBO1 walker of that mod (walking 8 directions x 22 frames, aiming 18 directions; files referenced from Yuoki, not copied); without it they keep the tinted sprites.
    - Rank droids have a star per rank in their icon and over them.
  Locale:
    - Messages are localised (en, ru); descriptions match the current behaviour. New droids, ranks, tooltips, buttons, panels, signals, research and the repair setting in en and ru; de/pl/pt-BR fall back to English for the new strings.
  Modding:
    - Rank prototypes, tooltips (computed from the final prototypes) and the entity filters of the command tool are made in data-updates (prototypes/ranks.lua), after Bob's drones exist; script/droids.lua builds the runtime name tables from the prototypes; new modules script/ranks.lua, script/pods.lua, script/territory.lua.
    - Code split into lib/shared.lua, prototypes/*.lua and script/*.lua; no globals are written in any stage. Recipes and technologies are created in data-updates so that the items and technologies of Bob's, Angels and Paranoidal exist; optional dependencies on bobwarfare, bobplates, angelssmelting and zzzparanoidal set the load order (Yuoki needs no dependency: only its files are referenced when it is active).
    - Removed the unused stdlib, 30log, robolib, config, migrations, WARP.md, QRF_Testing_Commands.txt, .gitignore, unused images (low resolution sprites, patrol pole, settings module, deployable item icons, settings signals, pick-up tool), and prototype fields that 2.0 does not read (destroy_action, idle, icon_mipmaps, is_deployer, item_slot_count, base_area/base_level, stackable, hr_version, ammo_type category).
    - ai_settings use the 2.0 field destroy_when_commands_fail.

Version: 2.4.27
Date: 2025.10.06
  Major Features:
    - Added Quick Reaction Force (QRF) system - squads automatically respond to building destruction within configurable range
    - QRF squads attack threat area, then return to their original positions and resume previous behavior
    - New runtime setting "QRF Response Distance" (0-500 tiles, default 500) - adjustable in-game without restart
  Settings:
    - Fixed mod loading error caused by incorrectly placed int-setting in data phase
    - Moved QRF distance setting to proper settings.lua file with full localization support
    - Added mod setting descriptions in English locale
  Stability:
    - Added comprehensive crash protection for pick-up tool interactions during QRF operations
    - Implemented protected API calls (pcall) for all unit group operations to prevent invalid commandable errors
    - Added squad member count validation to prevent operations on empty squads
    - Enhanced error handling with graceful QRF state cleanup when squads become invalid
  Quality of Life:
    - Added detailed QRF debug messages for testing and troubleshooting
    - Created comprehensive testing command file (QRF_Testing_Commands.txt) with console commands
    - Improved QRF state management with proper cleanup in all edge cases
