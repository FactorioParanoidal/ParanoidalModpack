Version: 9.1.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Changes:
    - Logistics: new row "Crashed logistic robots" (total and last hour) when paranoidal-robot-attrition is active (its remote get_force_summary).
    - World: days passed come from the NightBrightness calendar (midnights since the landing, the same count as EvoUI and the achievements); new rows "Year on the planet" (with the day of the year) and "Season" (in the player's name style).
  Major Features:
    - PMRPGsystem is renamed to paranoidal-CodexRPG (Paranoidal Codex RPG). PMRPGsystem 9.0.5 stays in the modpack as a bridge: when this mod is added to an old save, it takes over the experience, levels, skills, team experience, kills and statistics once, and the windows and buttons of the old version are removed. Settings, keys and the remote interface "RPG" keep their names.
  Features:
    - The RPG window is now the base journal: a book over most of the screen with bookmarks on the left and a two-page spread (one page on narrow screens). Sizes follow the screen resolution and interface scale and stay the same on every bookmark; long texts wrap instead of being cut. I opens the character pages, Alt+I the last statistics page, Esc closes.
    - Character: the old panel laid out again (level, experience, skills as a list with their bonus, pick-up option), plus own kills, RPG commands, travels on foot, by car and by train, causes of death, hand crafting, trees and rocks.
    - Base passport: status, twelve main numbers, latest milestones, a map and the summary as plain text for chats.
    - Base: status of the base by science packs reached, production, research with science per minute for each pack, power (now, hourly average, 10-hour peak, sources, consumers), logistics, most built buildings and most made items, joke indices.
    - World: charted and generated chunks of Nauvis, area, farthest charted chunk, chunks under the base, time of day and days passed, a minimap, the ruin journal (AbandonedRuins-paranoidal), resources, wood and fish.
    - War: kills and kills per hour, ammunition used, what killed the enemies, losses, evolution (says when it grows from research), pollution now and over the game, leaderboard.
    - Quality: production split by quality, best quality, defect share among items that can be defective, quality control level and refining workshop counters (ic-more-qualities).
    - Milestones: first drill, generator, lab, turret, radar, car, locomotive, train stop, rocket, research with each new science pack and more, with a chat message (per-player setting "Announce base milestones"). On older saves milestones reached earlier get a time estimated from the statistics graphs ("≈ 3:20"), or "by" with the update time when the graphs do not tell.
    - Paranoidal milestones: first crushed ore, basic circuit board, steel, molten metal, electronic circuit, crude oil, plastic, sulfuric acid, aluminium, battery, advanced circuit, titanium, silicon wafer, processing unit and uranium-235; first refining workshop, quality control post, recycler, Factorissimo building and spidertron; first quality module and first items of the main quality tiers; first ruins found, first legendary ruin found and beaten. The time of a first product comes from the statistics graphs.
    - Codex: short notes on defects, quality, ruins, experience and how the statistics are counted.
    - Remote interface RPG.get_base_snapshot(force), RPG.refresh_base_snapshot(force) and RPG.get_base_counters(force) (milestones and kills by cause).
  Optimizations:
    - Nothing is checked every tick. The base snapshot is built only while a statistics page is open, at most once a minute, by a short job with a fixed number of engine calls per tick that stops when done. Counters use events this mod already handles, building events filtered to the milestone entity types and the player's tile changes. Milestone times on older saves are estimated once, when the save is first loaded with this version.
    - First products and ruins are checked every 10 minutes (a few dozen reads of the statistics); quality tiers only while the statistics are counted.
  Locale:
    - ru: skill names in sentence case, the broken robot hint and the "trash slots" typo fixed, shorter skill hints, the player tag tooltip translated.
  Bugfixes:
    - Milestone chat messages show icons only: their [item=...]-like tags are printed as [img=...], so the chat no longer adds "[Virtual signal: Check mark]"-style names after them.
