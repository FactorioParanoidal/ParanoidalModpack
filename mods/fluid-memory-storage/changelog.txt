Version: 9.8.0
Date: 2026-10-14
  Optimizations:
    - The periodic update visits only the units of the current slot (unit numbers bucketed by lag_id, rebuilt when units are added or removed) instead of walking every unit.
  Features:
    - Version number starts with 9 (Paranoidal fork): the Mod Portal never offers an "update" over local changes.
    - The fluid can be chosen in the GUI before it arrives
    - Reset button for an empty unit
    - Quality of the unit lowers its power usage along a parabola, down to 20% for the highest quality
    - Exact amount in the tooltip
  Changes:
    - Mixing the stored fluid with fluid of another temperature keeps the lower temperature instead of a weighted average
    - The GUI closes when the player walks out of reach; remote view is read-only
    - Everything stored is lost when the unit is destroyed (now stated in the description)
    - The amount kept in the tank is half of the tank's own volume, never more
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed the stored temperature drifting: the whole tank was mixed into the store on every update
    - Fixed a crash when a packed unit with a removed fluid was placed; renamed fluids are followed for packed units too
    - Fixed lost hidden parts deleting the whole unit; they are recreated
    - Fixed cloning creating the power source of the item memory unit and duplicating hidden parts with clone_area
    - Fixed the GUI showing 0 °C when the tank was empty
    - Fixed the power usage setting applying only after the next content change
    - Fixed a stale GUI frame after loading a save
  Optimizations:
    - Build, mine and clone events are filtered by name
    - Units removed without an event (chunk or surface deletion) are cleaned up silently
