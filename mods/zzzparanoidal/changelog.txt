Empty pipes retain the last fluid; exhaustion selects the next matching resource. Dry ores are selected only before any fluid has been identified. Fuel fluid is excluded, fluid-output extractors are unchanged, and filter changes never replace drills or delete fluids. Registered drills are checked over a 120-tick wheel; depletion wakes nearby managed drills on the next tick, before AutoDeconstruct checks them. Init/configuration changes register existing drills. Old saves: a JSON migration turns all 205 Cursed-FMD variants (drill___resource, 12 of them for Creative ore) into the plain drill of the same type with quality, health, modules, fuel, wires, ghosts and blueprints kept. A former dry-resource variant keeps no fluid input (the engine fixes this when the drill is built): it goes on mining its dry ore and needs rebuilding to switch to fluid-requiring ore.
    - Automatic-Cursed-FMD respects a manual drill filter: any filter other than the one it applied (another ore, blacklist mode, extra slots, pasted or blueprinted settings) is left untouched, so the player can choose between ores sharing one fluid. Clearing the filter returns the drill to automatic selection.
    - Automatic-Cursed-FMD waits on an ore that lies under the drill: with no fluid yet and no dry ore, or with a fluid no ore under it needs, the filter is set to the first fluid-requiring ore of the mining area (name order) instead of the first fluid-requiring ore of the whole game, so a new drill on bobmonium no longer shows sapphirite. Without its own fluid that ore is not mined; the drill shows the real ore and the missing fluid. Only when no fluid-requiring ore is under the drill does the old placeholder remain. Drills already waiting on a wrong ore are corrected on their next check (no migration); fluids inside are kept. A waiting drill keeps its witness until the fluid changes or a drill event (rotation, flush, paste) arrives.
    - Added an isolated, optional liquid fuel balance for flamethrower turrets, Hero ranks and the flamethrower wagon: six explicit fuels including diesel, damage multipliers from the agreed final fuel values; all nine gases from the source fuel list are excluded. Native streams, fire, target burning, refuelling, timing and research handle combat without Lua shot or hit handlers. Disabling the startup setting restores the source fuel lists and multipliers. Save migration clears only unsupported internal fuel and preserves external pipes and existing turrets.
