Version: 8.1.28
  Changes:
    - Technology list and its search show every level of chains whose levels unlock new buildings or items (upgrade = false): automation 7-9, electronics assembling machines 4-5, electric steel furnace, Schall tanks, battery electric train chargers and fuel, pickup towers, smog protection, Angels ironworks and alloy smelting. Before, the list showed only the current level of such a chain, so automation 7-9 could not be found until automation 6 was researched. Bonus chains stay collapsed.
    - Vehicle power equipment and engines are always standard quality (user decision 2026-11-05): every recipe of vehicle solar panels 1-5, vehicle fission cells 1-6, vehicle fuel cells 2x2/3x3/4x4, vehicle fission reactors 1-6, vehicle nuclear reactor, vehicle batteries 1-6 and 1-2, both vehicle engines and vehicle belt immunity equipment is in the registry "ic-normal-result" of ic-more-qualities, like seeds: no quality modules, a positive result becomes standard, the control post does not take them. Equipment already made at a quality keeps it.
    - Sawdust balance: the sawdust fuel brick recipe now makes wood pellets (8 sawdust -> 1 pellet, 2 s, same technology); charcoal from sawdust: 50 sawdust -> 11 charcoal, 33 s (was 24 -> 5, 15 s); charcoal from wood pellets: 4 -> 7, 4 s (was 2 -> 3, 2 s); wood pellets from cellulose fiber take 16 fiber instead of 12 (no energy gain). Fuel pollution: sawdust 200% (was 140%), wood 250% (was 175%), wood pellets 150% (Beta 8 110%, was 100% after the ID rename), cellulose fiber 185% (was 140%).
    - Solid fuel: vehicle acceleration 110% (was 105%) and top speed 105% (was 100%). Coke pellets stay better (110% / 120%).
    - Diesel fuel recipe: 25 liquid fuel instead of 20 (with 20 methane and 1 mineral catalyst -> 25 diesel fuel). The step multiplied fuel energy by 1.66, now by 1.42; diesel fuel keeps 2.25 MJ.
    - Diesel generator: burns only diesel fuel (was any fuel fluid) at 85% efficiency (was 100%); full 4 MW takes about 2.1 diesel fuel per second.
    - Battery electric locomotive and maglev locomotive: quality may change their top speed (quality_affects_max_speed), like every other locomotive of the build. The battery electric locomotive copied the vanilla one before the quality DLC set the flag, the maglev locomotive is defined from scratch without it. Their hidden multiple-unit twins get it too. Used by the locomotive quality bonus of ic-more-qualities (top speed up to x2 at the highest quality).
    - MIRV2 ballistic missile silo: price between MIRV 2.1.0 and the rocket silo of the build (200 concrete, 75 electric engines, 50 processing units, 50 titanium pipes, 250 nitinol, 100 advanced processing units, 25 low density structures, 50 heat shield tiles, 50 assembling machines 6); in the combat menu next to the M.I.R.V.
    - First turrets: gun turret damage modifier 2.0 (was 1.5), Bob gun turret tiers 3-5 3.5 / 5.0 / 6.5 (was 3.0 / 4.0 / 5.0, tier 2 stays 2.0); standard darts deal 2 physical (was 3) and keep 2 piercing, so the wooden dart turret and the dart rifle hit softer. Rifle and turret share the ammo (user decision 2026-11-06).
  Compatibility:
    - Factorio 2.1: the flare stack and clarifier get one output slot so the engine accepts their void recipes with a zero-probability item result. Restores automatic gas and water disposal; the recipes still produce no items.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: assembler pipe pictures are taken from __base__/prototypes/entity/assembler-pictures.lua (2.1 removed the global assembler2pipepictures/assembler3pipepictures and assemblerpipes.lua).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: battery electric locomotive and wagon no longer set crash_trigger (2.1 removed the base global crash_trigger(); it was a silent sound).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: recycling recipe generation and recycling icons are taken from the built-in recycler mod (__recycler__/recycling.lua, __recycler__/graphics/icons/); recycling code checks mods["recycler"] instead of mods["quality"].
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: science pack checks accept every item type (2.1 vanilla science packs are plain items, mod packs may stay tools).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: the Beta 8 icon list addresses the vanilla science packs as items (they were tools in 2.0).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: defines.inventory.assembling_machine_*, furnace_* and rocket_silo_input/output/modules replaced by defines.inventory.crafter_input/output/modules/trash.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: the recycling rebuild reads recipe categories as a list (the first is the former main category) and independent_probability; rebuilt recycling recipes stay available from the start as in 2.0 (the 2.1 generator locks them behind the recycling technology), and the hidden recycling unlocks (also those left by removed recipes) are dropped from technologies.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: recipes use categories (RecipePrototype::category and additional_categories were merged into categories; the first entry keeps the meaning of the former main category, a changed main category keeps the other entries).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: recipe category changes go through bobmods.lib.recipe.set_main_category/get_category; the Beta 8 energy data still uses the 2.0 names and is converted on apply (category -> categories[1], fuel_category -> fuel_categories); fuel barrels use fuel_categories.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: the recipe category basic-crafting (removed from the 2.1 base game) is kept: it is created and given back to the vanilla assembling machines 1-3 as in 2.0 (mining drill bits).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: the recycling rebuild replaces only pure recycling recipes (every category recycling), as 2.0 replaced only category recycling; scrap recycling (recycling + hand-crafting) stays.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: product probability renamed to independent_probability (recipe results, minable results, generated products).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: loot entries use the 2.1 format (type, name, amount_min, amount_max, independent_probability instead of item, count_min, count_max, probability).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: items use fuel_categories (ItemPrototype::fuel_category was removed).
    - Factorio 2.1 quality tips: the simulations roll further tiers with chain_probability of the tier reached (2.1; the 2.0 next_probability), show the module effect as it is (2.0 showed it divided by 10) and the ladder column "upgrade chance" is chain_probability, so the tips texts (effect x 10 x upgrade chance 0.204 ...) stay right.
    - Factorio 2.1: removed tweaks/custom/uniform-recipies.lua and the recipe fields always_show_products/show_amount_in_title of the Beta 8 data (removed in 2.1, silently ignored); the wooden pipe sets hide_connection_info on its pipe connections (moved there from the fluid box); the loader counterpart rows of a deconstruction planner are appended after its last filter (2.1 filter counts are dynamic: entity_filter_count is the highest used index, engine probe test-runs/port21-decon-20261102; the 2.0 code saw a planner without holes as full).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision D30 A): every assembling machine and furnace with fluid boxes can be flipped as in 2.0 (use_mirroring = true unless the machine sets it, tweaks/entity/crafting-machine-mirroring.lua).
    - Factorio 2.1 graphics: the torch flame uses the 2.0 refinery fire kept in angelspetrochemgraphics (the base file was removed in 2.1).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision G2 A): spawners work as in 2.0 - no spawner gets the 2.1 acid cloud when its spawning is blocked (spawn_blocked_trigger), and the copies of the vanilla spawner (biter and spitter spawners, bobenemies tier 0, Big-Monsters spawner and volcano) spawn within the 2.0 radius 10 and spacing 3 instead of 2 and 1 (tweaks/entity/spawners-2-0.lua).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision G20 A): the recycling rebuild keeps the 2.0 recycling times (time of the source recipe / 16; self-recycling: of the recipe named like the item, else 0.5 s, / 16); the 2.1 generator divides them by the result count of the source recipe. Results and yields are unchanged.
    - Factorio 2.1 graphics: the graphics atlas no longer swaps the Reskins oil refinery icon base and the signal-science-pack icon for base-game files that 2.1 redrew (the swap was only valid while the pixels were equal).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision G21): new maps get the 2.1 vanilla maximum enemy expansion group size 10 instead of 20 (tweaks/custom/map-gen-presets.lua). In 2.1 a settler group has random(min, max) * evolution_group_size_factor ^ evolution members (vanilla factor 8), so the 2.0 value 20 gave groups twice the 2.1 vanilla size; the engine itself sets 10 in loaded 2.0 saves, so new maps and migrated saves now match.
    - Factorio 2.1 CI: the base dependency is base >= 2.1.24 (the CI launchability check downloads the headless server of exactly that version; 2.0.77 refuses mods with factorio_version 2.1).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision G22 A): wagons have no max_speed (tweaks/entity/wagon-max-speed-2-1.lua, also over the values of tweaks/entity/trains.lua and the other mods). 2.1 takes a train's max speed as the average max_speed of the rolling stock that sets it (2.0: the minimum of the whole train), so the wagon values of the build raised trains above the speed of their locomotives; now a train goes at the speed of its locomotives, as in 2.0 while the wagons were not slower.
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision G23 A): the recycling rebuild recycles as in 2.0 - the pistol is decomposed into its ingredients (vanilla 2.1 hides its recipe and sets auto_recycle = false), and recycle_to_ingredients_of (new in 2.1, set by vanilla on hazard concrete and refined hazard concrete) is dropped from every recipe, so an item is decomposed into the ingredients of its own recipe.
    - Factorio 2.1: the hiend fluid wagon gets the valve heights of the vanilla 2.1 fluid wagon of the same size (base_valve_z_offset_projected_when_horizontal/vertical -1.4/-1.55, used to draw the pump arm); without them it took the 2.1 defaults -1.375/-0.65. The other valve offsets are the 2.1 defaults, equal to the vanilla ones.
