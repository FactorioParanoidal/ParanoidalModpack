---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-10
  Major Features:
    - First version (Space Age branch of Paranoidal, user request 2026-11-10): a tester panel opened by /sa-test (optionally with a tab name), Ctrl+Shift+T or the shortcut bar button, for every player; the map setting "Tester panel available" switches it off. Adds no items, recipes or technologies.
    - Playthrough tab: every point after the rocket (the rocket silo, each technology after it that discovers a place or unlocks a science pack) as a button - click researches it with every missing prerequisite, Shift+click only the prerequisites, right click shows it; any technology with what is still missing and the action that researches a trigger technology; undo with dependents, finish the current research, fast labs, research everything.
    - Planets tab: every place of the star map (hidden ones on request) with unlock/lock travel, go there (the surface is created and generated, a landfill pad if there is no ground), look at it in map view and move the chosen platform to it; platforms: create with the starter pack, enter (the platform flies to the player first if needed), leave the hub, pause, kit to the hub, asteroid chunks past the hub, an asteroid strike, delete.
    - Items tab: quality (every quality of the build, defects included) and amount; any item to the inventory or an infinite chest, any fluid to an infinite pipe at a chosen temperature, another stack of the item in hand, the game's test devices, clear inventory; a kit of every planet built from its technologies and its surface (buildings to the inventory, raw materials, parts and packs into infinite chests, fluids into infinite pipes, packs to the inventory); a platform kit.
    - Modes tab: instant crafting, immortality, fast running, long reach, fast mining (bonuses are added and taken back exactly, so bonuses of other mods stay; a new body after death gets them again), heal and repair, the map editor; for the surface the player looks at: eternal day, peaceful mode, power without wires (global electric network with a test energy source), enemy evolution here and its reset, remove enemies nearby, clear an area, pave a 32x32 pad with any tile, chart around, chart everything, generate around; game speed (pause, x1...x30) and enemy evolution on/off.
    - Checks tab: what is under the cursor (name, internal name, which mod created and changed it, health, status, recipe, rocket parts, amount, frozen, temperature, fluids, surface conditions met or not) with repair, rocket ready and remove; where and how a recipe is made (unlocked or not, which technologies unlock it, ingredients and results, surface conditions, every building of its categories green if it works on this surface); properties of the current surface next to Nauvis, day length, solar power, pollution, platform state; resources within 256 tiles; a bug report file in script-output.
    - Actions that change the shared game (research, places, platforms, speed, enemies, surface modes) are announced to every player with the name of who did it; dangerous ones (undo research, research everything, delete a platform, clear inventory, remove) need a second click.
    - The bug report message gives the full path of the file (%APPDATA%\Factorio-2.1-SA\script-output\paranoidal-sa-tester\report-<tick>.txt, and the one of a regular install).
    - Remote interface "paranoidal-sa-tester" (open, close, is_open, run, click, set_choice, select_tab, elements, actions, catalog) for test scenarios.
