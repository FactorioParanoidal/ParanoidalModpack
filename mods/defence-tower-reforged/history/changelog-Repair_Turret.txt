Version: 9.0.6
Date: 2026-10-21
  Optimisations:
    - No more on_entity_damaged: its cost grew with every hit anywhere on the map, even without turrets. Repairable buildings inside turret areas are kept once in a list and a small portion of it (8-150 entities) is polled each tick, a full pass in about 1 s. New buildings in an area are added on build; each turret re-reads its area slowly as a fallback. Vehicles and characters are searched in each turret area every 2 s. Without turrets the mod does no work per tick.

Version: 9.0.5
Date: 2026-10-21
  Changes:
    - Added three upgrade branches after the repair turret technology: repair speed (10 levels, +20% each), beams (4 levels, +1 simultaneous target each, every level needs the next science pack type) and range (10 levels, +1 tile each).
    - Each beam heals its own target. Energy (50 kJ per HP) and repair pack durability per HP are unchanged, so consumption grows with the upgrades; the energy buffer is raised per force to fit one full volley.
    - The repair area is drawn by the script for the hovering player with the researched range; removed the static radius visualisation.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash when loading a save with an older version if another mod (e.g. Shortcuts) resets technology effects before the repair turret rebuilds its data. Events are ignored until the rebuild.

Version: 9.0.4
Date: 2026-10-20
  Changes:
    - Paranoidal fork of 2.0.4. Repair only: the turret is no longer a roboport, it has no logistic network, robot construction area or robot charging, and it no longer builds ghosts or deconstructs.
    - Removed the original upgrade technologies (repair turret power 1-3, efficiency 1-3, construction). The turret technology needs gun turrets and costs 250 automation science packs.
    - Repair range is 7 tiles from the centre (14x14 tile square), shown on hover and while placing. Removed the repair range and hide paths settings.
    - Power: 500 kW idle, 1.5 MW in total while repairing (1 MJ buffer). Power comes from a hidden energy entity next to the turret.
    - Repair packs are taken only from the turret's own slot. One pack repairs as much as by hand (speed x durability HP). An icon shows when a target is waiting and the slot is empty.
    - 20 HP/s: one shot every 31 ticks, about 10.3 HP per shot. The heal is applied directly; the beam is only visual.
    - Runtime rewritten: one control.lua without global helpers, native event filters (enemies, nature and robots are not processed), only active turrets are updated, damaged vehicles and characters are checked every 61 ticks.
    - Moving the turret with Even Pickier Dollies is supported. On configuration change all turrets and their energy entities are rebuilt from the map; turrets of the original roboport version are removed by the game when the prototype type changes.
    - Added Russian locale. Removed the turret gift for the player named Klonan.
