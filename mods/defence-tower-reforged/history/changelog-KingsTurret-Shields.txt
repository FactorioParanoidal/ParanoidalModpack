Version: 9.111.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Major Features:
    - Shield generators replace the shields of every single turret. A generator (2×2 building, unlocked by the base research) protects the eligible turrets of its force whose centre is within 25 tiles (setting) with one shared energy buffer charged by the electric network. It does not protect itself.
    - A hit that would destroy a protected turret is absorbed: the generator pays the whole hull and the turret survives. Other damage stays until the turret is repaired from the generator: every 3 ticks while it keeps being damaged, up to every 300 ticks when it is not.
    - A turret inside several generators is paid for by the one with the highest charge fraction; when it runs dry the next one pays. An emptied generator stops for 10 ticks.
  Optimizations:
    - The mod gets only the hits that would destroy a protected turret (damage filter on the final health), not every hit on every turret. On the stress save the per-turret shields took about 4.9 ms per tick (3.2 ms of it the engine handing the hits to the mod).
    - Idle generators and turrets cost no Lua beyond a look every 300 ticks per protected turret and every 30 ticks per generator; payments of one tick share one buffer read and write per generator.
    - 676 hidden buffer prototypes are kept, but there is one buffer per generator instead of one per turret.
    - Stress save (bench, 2026-10-21): about 0.3 ms per tick of mod code during the full fight (the per-turret shields: 1.7 ms of code plus 3.2 ms of engine event delivery).
  Changes:
    - The generator pays the damage the hull took (after resistances), not the raw damage of the hit.
    - Turrets under fire show their real health between repairs; construction robots may repair them too.
    - Shield bars and bubbles on every turret are gone; the generator shows a charge bar, a "no power" icon and, in Alt mode, its circle (blue, orange when low, red when empty). An absorbed hit shows the bubble on the turret.
    - The shield toggle takes the selected turrets out of the generators' protection (Shift gives it back).
    - New startup settings: generator radius and turret shares per generator (capacity, recharge and drain of one generator are those of 20 turret shields of 9.110 by default). Generator price: 20 steel plates, 20 electronic circuits, 20 iron gear wheels, 40 copper cables.
    - The research descriptions now speak of generators. The long fluid turret bars are removed.
  Info:
    - No migration from 9.110.x (never released): the per-turret shield entities go with their prototypes, the stored state and the render objects of the mod are dropped on load. Turrets are unprotected until generators are built.

Version: 9.110.1
Date: 2026-10-21
  Optimizations:
    - Stress save (216 shielded turrets, ~417 hits per tick, mostly Rampant acid puddles): mod time 4.8 -> 1.6 ms per tick (in-game profiler, same simulation).
    - A turret hit again within 60 ticks is healed to 0.01 below its maximum health; the missing 0.01 is given back 60 ticks after its last hit. Making a damaged entity fully healthy costs the engine about 5.7 us, other health writes about 0.2 us; this was half of the mod time. A single hit after a quiet period is still healed to full at once.
    - The shield bar and the hit bubble are drawn once per turret and then only changed (sprite, time to live) instead of being destroyed and drawn again on every hit and every recharge step.
    - The visual side of hits (bar, bubble, recharge look) runs at most once per 5 ticks per turret.
  Changes:
    - The bar counts every square that holds any charge: a nearly full shield under fire shows a steady full bar. A bar shown full disappears 41 ticks after the last hit or after the shield became full.
    - Under continued fire the bubble keeps looping and is extended shortly before it ends instead of restarting every 5 ticks; the second, nearly identical sprite sheet is removed.
    - Shield toggle: the tooltip says that Shift (alt-select) gives the shields back to the selected turrets.
  Bugfixes:
    - Records of 9.110.0 are migrated also when only the code was replaced without a version change (first handler after loading).
  Info:
    - Checked on the stress save: a single hit larger than the turret health is absorbed, the turret survives.

Version: 9.110.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Major Features:
    - Paranoidal fork for Factorio 2.0 (major version 9 against portal updates). Runtime rewritten for bases with thousands of turrets.
  Optimizations:
    - An idle or recharging shield costs no Lua time: the electric network fills the hidden energy interface. The every-tick handler, the 60-tick pass over all charging shields and the per-turret graphics rebuilds are gone.
    - A hit on a shielded turret is O(1); hits on turrets without a shield end after one table lookup. Damage events are filtered to ammo, electric and fluid turrets (worms are no longer delivered to the handler).
    - The shield bar is one render object attached to the turret; only its sprite changes when the step changes. Recharging shields are looked at once per bar step (tick buckets), less often without power.
    - The hit effect is drawn by LuaRendering instead of creating and destroying a smoke entity per hit.
    - Research upgrades, enabling and rescans go through a work queue (25 shield entities per tick) instead of rebuilding every shield of the force in one tick.
    - on_tick is registered only while that schedule or queue has work.
  Bugfixes:
    - Rescans no longer create duplicate shield entities and orphaned bars (records were overwritten without destroying the old interface).
    - Rescans no longer give shields to turrets of every force: the force filter was passed as a positional value and ignored.
    - Removed the never-cleared fluid turret orientation list that grew the save.
    - Shield levels are counted correctly at the last research level and for research finished before the shield technology.
    - Turrets removed by any means (including destroy without an event) lose their shield via on_object_destroyed.
    - The shield toggle actually switches shields off (in 1.1 it only drew an icon) and switching off stops the power draw.
    - Area clones no longer leave ownerless shield entities; a cloned turret keeps the shield energy of its source.
    - A turret replaced by a script in the same place and tick (HeroTurrets promotion) keeps its shield energy and its off state; this replaces the 1-second damage immunity of new turrets.
    - Teleported turrets (Picker Dollies) take their shield with them.
    - Hidden helper turrets (Bio Industries arboretum area, hidden fakes) never get shields.
  Changes:
    - Removed the turret shield combinator: its logic was gone since 1.100.0, the item did nothing.
    - The shield toggle is a shortcut (spawned tool, no recipe): select = off, alt-select = on.
    - Settings renamed to kts-*; "Shields need research" is a startup setting now (it changes prototypes).
    - Shield entity and graphics names prefixed with kts- (no clash with Shield-FX, which uses the same artwork).
    - Chat commands "kts reset" and "shieldstats" replaced by /kts-rebuild (admin) and /kts-stats.
    - Technology prerequisites: military science pack, chemical science pack and production science pack technologies for the packs the technologies use.
    - Drops the optional EnhancedGridEnergyStores dependency (1.1 only).
  Locale:
    - English rewritten for the new settings, Russian added.

Version: 1.100.0
Date: 9/5/2020
  Changes:
    - Kingdud forks this mod from the original and updates this changelog to work in-game. Any updates prior to this one that reference 'me' are not referring to Kingdud.
    - Greatly simplify backend code. Completely remove on_tick function call.
    - Remove ability to toggle power usage on/off
    - Remove ability to change most settings during gameplay (they are startup variables now).
    - Shields now use the 'energy_drain' property instead of 'power_usage', for the power drain method; basically, moving the drain to the in-game variable specifically designed to act as a drain!
    - Shields now use the internal buffer of the electric interface as the shield bar, and the inflow rate as the recharge rate. This allowed for complete removal of code that was micro-managing these actions.
    - Shields now use raw damage, rather than post-resist damage. So if you are using a modded turret with high resists, you may notice your shields now taking a LOT more damage, because your shields now have 0 resists, whereas before they were piggy-backing the resists of your turret.
    - Overall shield design is meant to be a meaningful, but modest, power draw while idle, and massive spikes (into the GW range with 1000s of turrets) during combat.
    - Removed the "No-power Penalty" from the mod. This functionality is now emulated by the energy_drain change. You lose the ability to drain power at two different rates, which never made any sense anyway.
    - Mod now uses the technology max_level property to limit infinite research to levels the mod is designed for (100 levels).
