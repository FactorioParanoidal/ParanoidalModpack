Version: 9.113.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Optimizations:
    - Reading tower squares (towers built or moved, range research, the shield switched off) is split into strips of 16 tiles and queued: at most 8 strips per tick for the repair side and 8 searches per tick for the shield. Many towers built at once or a research with hundreds of towers no longer stop the game for one long tick.
    - A range research reads only the ring between the old and the new square. When the square shrinks, the shield drops the links outside it without any search; turrets inside keep their shield records and their look schedule.
    - "Shield generation" and its capacity/regeneration levels switch the towers of the force a few per tick.
    - Damaged vehicles and characters are searched once per covered chunk (the union of the tower squares in it) instead of once per tower; a chunk where nothing moves is searched less and less often (every 2 s up to every 16 s), a chunk with vehicles every 2 s.
    - Buildings in tower squares are polled in two tiers: all of them about every 3 s (was every 1 s), the buildings of chunks with recent damage (found by the polls, the vehicle search or a shield intercept/repair) every 15 ticks for 10 s after the last damage. Small lists are no longer polled 8 entities per tick.
    - Only buildings whose centre is inside a tower square are watched.
    - A tower re-reads its square for buildings created without events every 30 s (was every 5 s), in strips.
    - An undamaged turret under a shield is looked at every 30 s (was every 5 s). A hit that would kill it is still intercepted at once; only chip damage outside fights is repaired later.
  Changes:
    - First damage of a building outside a fight is noticed after up to about 3 s (was 1 s); inside hot chunks after up to 15 ticks.

Version: 9.112.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Major Features:
    - Defence Tower Reforged: one Paranoidal mod built from Repair Turret 9.0.6 (Klonan, Paranoidal fork) and King's Turret Shields 9.111.0 (Kingdud, Paranoidal fork). Both former mods are removed from the build; their histories are in history/.
    - The repair turret is now the defense tower. It keeps its building, graphics, repair packs, beams, power and the repair upgrade branches (speed, beams, range); the IDs are kept, so towers and researched levels of saves stay.
    - New research "Shield generation" (after the defense tower and the chemical science pack, 500 × automation, logistic, chemical and military science packs): every defense tower of the force protects the turrets in its square with an energy shield. A hit that would destroy a turret is absorbed, turret damage is repaired from the shield without repair packs. The tower does not protect itself.
    - The shield capacity and regeneration branches (25 levels each, formulas of 1.1) now follow "Shield generation"; regeneration still needs the production science pack.
  Changes:
    - One zone for repair and shield: the tower square, ±7 tiles from the centre, +1 per range research level. The shield generator building, its radius setting and its recipe are removed.
    - Turrets inside a shielded tower square are left to the shield: the repair beams and repair packs only repair other buildings, vehicles and characters, and turrets switched out of the shield with the toggle.
    - The shield has its own hidden energy buffer under the tower, separate from the repair energy: an empty shield does not stop repairs and repairs do not take shield energy. Its charge bar and "no power" icon are shown on the tower.
    - A turret inside several towers is paid for by the tower with the highest shield charge; the next one pays when it runs dry.
    - Setting "Turret shares per tower shield" (default 20: one tower shield = 20 turret shields of 1.1).
    - Commands /dtr-stats and /dtr-rebuild; remote interface "defence-tower".
  Info:
    - No migration: saves keep their towers and research; the shield state of King's Turret Shields and its generators are dropped.
