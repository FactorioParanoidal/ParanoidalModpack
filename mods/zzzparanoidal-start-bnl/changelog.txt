Version: 2.0.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Major Features:
    - Renamed from zzzparanoidal-shipwreck to zzzparanoidal-start-bnl ("PARANOIDAL: Start with BnL"): the whole start of the modpack is in this mod. The BnL landing message of zzzparanoidal moved here. Prototype names and the start level setting keep their names.
    - Falling debris: for about 10 minutes stragglers of the ship fall around the crash site from the west-south-west; a chat warning (no mark on the ground), the roar of a low pass (from 5 s before the impact; players within 40 tiles of the impact point hear an incoming shell whistle ending at the impact instead) and the shadow of the piece (20-32 times larger and at 15% opacity at first, shrinking to the real shadow at full opacity at the impact) come before the impact, which blows up across the impact area (heard by every player at full volume wherever it falls), damages the area, shakes the camera and leaves a burning wreck with cargo and ammo. Sometimes a piece aims at a player building. One big piece carries the flight recorder.
    - Burning wrecks: almost every start wreck carries ammo; a fire next to it sets it burning, and about a third of the start wrecks catch fire in the first minute and a half after the landing. A burning wreck shows flames and smoke and loses health steadily until it burns out at a random 2-10% of its health; the ammo cooks off on the way (hurting the wreck and everyone around, throwing cargo out, lighting the ground) and the rest of it blows up when the fire goes out. The cargo and the mining loot whose quality can change lose one quality step every 10-15 s of fire (down to defect -5 at most); materials and items without defects stay normal. Each of these steps also burns away one whole random cargo stack with a 20% chance (shown over the wreck). A burning wreck cannot be destroyed by anything but its own fire and stays so after it. Wreck health x10 (the ship x1.25), wreck containers at least 4 slots.
    - Debris trail 500-800 tiles west-south-west with the detached compartments (new graphics built from the vanilla ship renders): reactor, medical bay, armory guarded by a nest, hydroponics, engineering bay on a lake shore (without a lake near, in its own crater pond); emergency beacons on the map.
    - Reactor compartment: gives nothing until repaired to full health; from the repair 15 MW, 5 MW at 5 minutes, then slowly down to zero at 60 minutes; radioactive while hot (fully while broken), cannot be mined or destroyed; then it is taken apart.
    - Flight recorder: researched in a lab (the recorder is the only ingredient), it writes the flight log of the Axiom-7 into the BnL journal, names the beacons, charts the previous expedition, gives two digits of the safe code and 20-30 red science packs.
    - BnL journal: a button and /bnl-journal keep every story message.
    - BnL insurance: after the first radar, supply pods (iron chests with one kind of items, sometimes advertising flyers) fall near the radar at growing intervals until the insurance ends or is cancelled by researching the logistic science pack.
    - Professions: after the landing each player chooses guard, geologist, mechanic, biologist or passenger (single player is paused while choosing); half-researched technologies and own kits, the mechanic has quality control 1-4 and doubles the start repair packs, the biologist gets seed recipes and a bio farm by pod at 10 minutes.
    - Landing anomalies (map setting): long night, magnetic storms, rich vein, quiet planet, dust storms.
    - Scrap (prototype and graphics from Factorio: Space Age): a deposit along the crash furrow, recycled by hand or in recyclers into gears, sticks, plates, cables, basic boards, repair packs and other parts.
    - The previous expedition (Axiom-6), 500-1000 tiles from the ship, with a note, defective machines and a damaged car; the ship turret becomes a crash veteran after 25 kills; the captain's safe opens for the BnL code given as circuit signals B, N, L; multiplayer escape pods; the distress beacon from three antenna parts and the BnL letters at milestones; the intro flight along the debris trail.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-18
  Bugfixes:
    - The ship and all its wrecks (vanilla and AAI) can be marked with the deconstruction planner; the vanilla ones were not deconstructable, the AAI containers lacked the player-creation flag. They stay not blueprintable.
  Optimizations:
    - Start fill and wreck-mining checks run every 5 ticks instead of every tick.
  Features:
    - Collects the whole start crash of Paranoidal: salvaged assembling machine, lab and generator with their graphics (from LootingSpaceshipWrecks), the AAI wrecks and their crash scene (from zzzcompability) and the wreck mining rules (from zzzparanoidal).
    - Startup setting with three start levels: minimum (half of the supplies, random defects on finished items that can be defective), medium (the previous supplies) and rich (default: the previous supplies with a random +1…+5 quality of every finished item).
    - One quality per item type per map; materials and intermediate products stay normal.
    - All electric poles get defects on the minimum level, also the ones exempt from the defect system.
    - Quality steps are weighted per level in shipwreck-config.lua; rich: normal 25%, +1 30%, +2 20%, +3 13%, +4 8%, +5 4%; minimum: normal 5%, −1 15%, −2 25%, −3 25%, −4 20%, −5 10%.
    - Belts, undergrounds, splitters and loaders are always normal quality.
    - Half of all start repair packs (rounded up, at least 10) are always normal quality without defects on every level, so the first refine workshop can be crafted; the rest gets the rolled quality.
    - Quality of the salvaged generator scales its power production and energy capacity.
  Changes:
    - The power production and energy capacity lines of the salvaged generator are shown once in the item tooltip (they were listed twice).
    - The player starts with an empty inventory; the former starting items are in the ship and its debris. Respawn items are unchanged.
    - Supplies of the ship, the debris and the AAI wrecks are placed as whole stacks by this mod instead of the freeplay item lists.
    - All wrecks and the ship use one balance table (shipwreck-config.lua), mining time 1.5 s and the pickaxe animation; the ship is mined in 6 s, big wrecks in 3 s.
    - The starting offshore pump is no longer part of the ship mining loot.
    - Ruins use the wreck prototypes with the medium loot and normal quality; the start level does not affect them.
