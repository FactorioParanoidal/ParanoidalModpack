Version: 2.0.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Major Features:
    - Renamed from zzzparanoidal-shipwreck to zzzparanoidal-start-bnl ("PARANOIDAL: Start with BnL"): the whole start of the modpack is in this mod. The BnL landing message of zzzparanoidal moved here. Prototype names and the start level setting keep their names.
    - Falling debris: for about 10 minutes stragglers of the ship fall around the crash site from the west-south-west; a chat warning (no mark on the ground), the roar of a low pass (from 5 s before the impact; players within 40 tiles of the impact point hear an incoming shell whistle ending at the impact instead) and the shadow of the piece (20-32 times larger and at 15% opacity at first, shrinking to the real shadow at full opacity at the impact) come before the impact, which blows up across the impact area (heard by every player at full volume wherever it falls), damages the area, shakes the camera and leaves a burning wreck with cargo and ammo. Sometimes a piece aims at a player building. One big piece carries the flight recorder.
    - Burning wrecks: almost every start wreck carries ammo; a fire next to it sets it burning, and about a third of the start wrecks catch fire in the first minute and a half after the landing. A burning wreck shows flames and smoke and loses health steadily until it burns out at a random 2-10% of its health; the ammo cooks off on the way (hurting the wreck and everyone around, throwing cargo out, lighting the ground) and the rest of it blows up when the fire goes out. The cargo and the mining loot whose quality can change lose one quality step at 75%, 50% and 25% health and one more when the fire goes out (4 steps, down to defect -5 at most); materials and items without defects stay normal. Each of these steps also burns away one whole random cargo stack with a 20% chance (shown over the wreck). A burning wreck cannot be destroyed by anything but its own fire and stays so after it. Wreck health x10 (the ship x1.25), wreck containers at least 4 slots.
    - Debris trail 500-800 tiles west-south-west with the detached compartments (new graphics built from the vanilla ship renders): reactor, medical bay, armory guarded by a nest, hydroponics, engineering bay on a lake shore (without a lake near, in its own crater pond); emergency beacons on the map.
    - Reactor compartment: repaired to full health it gives 10 MW falling along a parabola to 1 MW at 10 minutes and zero at about 14.6 minutes after the landing; radioactive while hot, cannot be mined or destroyed; then it is taken apart.
    - Flight recorder: researched in a lab (the recorder is the only ingredient), it writes the flight log of the Axiom-7 into the BnL journal, names the beacons, charts the previous expedition, gives two digits of the safe code and 20-30 red science packs.
    - BnL journal: a button and /bnl-journal keep every story message.
    - BnL insurance: after the first radar, supply pods (iron chests with one kind of items, sometimes advertising flyers) fall near the radar at growing intervals until the insurance ends or is cancelled by researching the logistic science pack.
    - Professions: after the landing each player chooses guard, geologist, mechanic, biologist or passenger (single player is paused while choosing); half-researched technologies and own kits, the mechanic has quality control 1-4 and doubles the start repair packs, the biologist gets seed recipes and a bio farm by pod at 10 minutes.
    - Landing anomalies (map setting): long night, magnetic storms, rich vein, quiet planet, dust storms.
    - Scrap (prototype and graphics from Factorio: Space Age): a deposit along the crash furrow, recycled by hand or in recyclers into gears, sticks, plates, cables, basic boards, repair packs and other parts.
    - The previous expedition (Axiom-6) with a note, defective machines and a damaged car; the ship turret becomes a crash veteran after 25 kills; the captain's safe opens for the BnL code given as circuit signals B, N, L; multiplayer escape pods; the distress beacon from three antenna parts and the BnL letters at milestones; the intro flight along the debris trail.
  Changes:
    - Security sergeant: also a shotgun and light armor (worn at once when the armor slot is empty); all the ammo in the start wrecks and the starliner (also the ammo of wrecks without an inventory) moves into the inventory of the first guard, the rest spills next to them.
    - The journal button on the top panel is the red and blue BnL logo on a transparent background (the button keeps its usual colour).
    - Falling debris has no red circle and warning sign at the impact point any more; insurance pods keep it.
    - Scrap deposit: the whole deposit holds a random 1000-10000 scrap (more near the ship), independent of the map settings.
    - The reactor compartment radiates through Radioactive-Damage-Reforged when it is loaded (its uranium damage, hazmat suit and Geiger counter: clicks within 20 tiles, damage within 12); without it the old damage every 5 s stays.
    - All event handlers go through one dispatcher; timed work runs in one on_nth_tick that exists only while something is scheduled, chunk/chart/selection/tick handlers only while needed.
    - New map settings for every part of the new start.
    - Hazardous cargo of the start wrecks (one kind per wreck instead of ammo only): ammo 40%, fuel barrels 20% (rare bursts, a pool of burning fuel), gas cylinders 10% (a burst cylinder flies 15-30 tiles and falls burning), high-tech fuel 10% (solid, enriched, high-octane, rocket, rarely nuclear: burns as the fire's fuel, x1.3 speed), explosives 5% (one big blast, cargo thrown far), oxygen 3% (jets of flame, x2 speed), batteries 2% (sparks, poison cloud), nothing 10%. At most half of it goes off during the fire and 30% of the rest in the last blast; what is left is loot (wrecks without an inventory add it to the mining loot). Bursts and the last blast hit player buildings within 6 tiles, the fire spreads to them.
    - Water barrels in a burning wreck slow the fire by half and burst into steam one by one; 2/3/5 of them (small/medium/big wreck) put it out without the last blast and the 4th quality step. The ship has a fire kit of 10 water barrels.
    - Fires have a character rolled on ignition: steady, smouldering (slow, thick smoke, keeps smoking after it is out), blazing (fast, throws embers that light the ground around) or flaring (flare-ups every 20-40 s); every burning wreck lights the ground around.
    - Torches over burning wrecks with fuel inside, from the vanilla oil refinery flame (the flame of the Angels flare stack) and the vanilla fire: fuel barrels burn out of three holes, gas as a flare stack column, high-tech fuel as one torch (taller for hotter fuel, nuclear the tallest), oxygen as a wild blaze; they shrink as the fuel runs out and go with the fire.
    - Buy n Large lore (WALL-E): the company is BnL everywhere, AUTO the autopilot, Directive A113 means no return, Shelby Forthright answers the SOS from a 2105 recording, Buy Yogurt, the trademarked north, lunch in a cup, blue is the new red; new turret names; all texts revised in RU and EN.
    - The Buy n Large logo (redrawn from scratch after the film's logo: red ellipse, B and L, the blue N circle): a weathered logo painted on the lower hull of the crashed starliner (also added to maps started earlier), on the compartments; a stencil on the escape pod, the corporate seal on the captain's safe and next to the BnL letters in the journal, a fresh logo on the insurance pods; the logo in the chat prefix and the journal title; the journal button is a BnL datapad.
    - Advertising flyers: four designs ("Everything must go! including you", the Axiom cruise, "BnL Life & Crash" insurance, a burnt one) on the ground and belts; an insurance pod of flyers quotes one of them in the chat.
    - The insurance radar and the distress beacon also count when built by a script (script_raised_built/revive).
    - Incoming shell whistle near the impact point: "Incoming mortar 1-4" by Zagge28 (freesound.org/s/241840, 241839, 241838, 241837, CC0 1.0), mono, normalized.
    - Falling debris sound: an excerpt of "airplane-boeing-flyby-right-to-left-a" by InspectorJ (freesound.org/people/InspectorJ/sounds/428086, CC BY 4.0), cut, mono, normalized.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-18
  Bugfixes:
    - The ship and all its wrecks (vanilla and AAI) can be marked with the deconstruction planner; the vanilla ones were not deconstructable, the AAI containers lacked the player-creation flag. They stay not blueprintable.
  Optimizations:
    - Start fill and wreck-mining checks run every 5 ticks instead of every tick.
  Features:
    - Collects the whole start crash of Paranoidal: salvaged assembling machine, lab and generator with their graphics (from LootingSpaceshipWrecks), the AAI wrecks and their crash scene (from zzzcompability) and the wreck mining rules (from zzzparanoidal).
    - Startup setting with three start levels: minimum (half of the supplies, random defects on finished items that can be defective), medium (the previous supplies) and rich (default: the previous supplies with a random +1…+5 quality of every finished item).
    - One quality per item type per map; materials and intermediate products stay normal.
    - All electric poles get defects on the minimum level, also the ones exempt from the defect system.
    - Quality steps are weighted per level in shipwreck-config.lua; rich: normal 25%, +1 30%, +2 20%, +3 13%, +4 8%, +5 4%; minimum: normal 5%, −1 15%, −2 25%, −3 25%, −4 20%, −5 10%.
    - Belts, undergrounds, splitters and loaders are always normal quality.
    - Half of all start repair packs (rounded up, at least 10) are always normal quality without defects on every level, so the first refine workshop can be crafted; the rest gets the rolled quality.
    - Quality of the salvaged generator scales its power production and energy capacity.
  Changes:
    - The power production and energy capacity lines of the salvaged generator are shown once in the item tooltip (they were listed twice).
    - The player starts with an empty inventory; the former starting items are in the ship and its debris. Respawn items are unchanged.
    - Supplies of the ship, the debris and the AAI wrecks are placed as whole stacks by this mod instead of the freeplay item lists.
    - All wrecks and the ship use one balance table (shipwreck-config.lua), mining time 1.5 s and the pickaxe animation; the ship is mined in 6 s, big wrecks in 3 s.
    - The starting offshore pump is no longer part of the ship mining loot.
    - Ruins use the wreck prototypes with the medium loot and normal quality; the start level does not affect them.
