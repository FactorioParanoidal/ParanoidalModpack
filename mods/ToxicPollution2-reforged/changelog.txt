---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Bugfixes:
    - The tooltip of the filter module and the closed-loop breathing module showed "Smog filtration" and "Power in smog" three times: the fields were set on both the item and the equipment, and the item tooltip also shows the fields of its equipment. They are now on the item only.
  Major Features:
    - Rewrite of toxicPollution2 1.0.0 (Toxic Pollution by Likos, ArtMares, ViridiVir) for Paranoidal. Replaces toxicPollution2; saves of the old mod are not migrated.
    - As in toxicPollution2, anything worn in the armor slot (respirator, gas mask, any armor) takes the whole harm of the smog on its resource: a protected player cannot die of smog until the resource is spent. The toxin resistance slows the wear (wear x (1 - resistance)); every armor except the respirator and the gas mask wears three times slower. At pollution 500 without research a respirator lasts about 6 min, gas mask 30 min, light armor 30 min, heavy armor 1.7 h, modular 4 h, power 9 h, power armor MK2 16 h. A stack in the armor slot is spent item by item.
    - Intoxication 0-100 % instead of the hidden x60 time multiplier, for the unprotected player: it grows while the inhaled pollution is above the tolerable level (150, +10 % per research level) and falls below it, faster in cleaner air and twice as fast near a working air filter, farm or greenhouse. Damage starts at 50 % and only while the player breathes smog: leaving the smog or putting on protection stops it at once (the old mod kept dealing the last damage after leaving, often lethal).
    - Vehicle cabin and breathing modules in the armor grid clean the air before it reaches the armor, so the armor lasts longer.
  Features:
    - Gas mask (resistance 80 %), filter module (50 %, 40 kW in smog) and closed-loop breathing module (100 %, 200 kW in smog). The respirator has resistance 50 % (was 5 %) and stays the start recipe; light armor (crafted from it) 50 % with 1000 resource.
    - Protection wears only in harmful air. Armor with an equipment grid is never destroyed: with the resource exhausted it stops protecting. An air filter (Carbon Capture) in the inventory refills the worn protection and returns a used filter.
    - Worn-out protection without a grid is replaced by the next one of the stack, then by the one from the inventory that lasts longest (the old mod chose the worst); the swap is silent.
    - Cabin filtration of vehicles: car 40 %, tank and aircraft 85 %, train 75 % (armoured 90 %), spidertron 95 %; shown in the vehicle tooltip.
    - No panel: one alert. "No protection from the toxic smog! Put on a respirator!" stays while the player breathes smog unprotected; the damage alert stays while the damage lasts; once, with a short danger sound, when the last protection (nothing left in the stack, no spare) is at 5 % or less than a minute before health damage; for about 10 s on entering smog in protection or when the smog gets twice as harmful. Its message holds the whole status: intoxication bar, pollution against tolerable, cabin and module filters, the resource bar, how long it lasts, spare air filters and the trend. The same status is printed by /toxic. Sounds per player setting.
    - Smog protection research: 10 levels instead of 25, each +10 % tolerable pollution and +10 % protection resource; levels 2, 5 and 8 unlock the gas mask and the modules.
    - Tips and tricks of the mod (moved from zzzparanoidal) with the real numbers. Command /toxic (status, kills) answers only the caller. Remote interface toxic_pollution.
    - Death from smog is announced to the force; no killer entity and no extra force any more.
  Changes:
    - Armor toxin resistance: Bob's power armor MK3/MK4/MK5 75/80/85 % (were 30/40/50, below MK2); Yuoki armor derives from it. Hazmat suit 85 % with 3000 resource (had 15 durability). Every armor without durability gets 10000 (creative armors stay infinite and never wear). Armor durability is shown as "Smog protection resource".
    - Pollution sensor: the signal "Pollution sensor" in a constant combinator. Only slots that hold the signal are written, only in manual sections, unchanged values are not rewritten; a combinator without the signal is forgotten (the old mod kept overwriting a slot the player had changed).
  Graphics:
    - New icons, equipment sprites, technology icon and thumbnail from free-licensed sources (see README): respirator, gas mask, GHS acute toxicity pictogram, Fluent Emoji. The biohazard sign is gone.
  Optimizations:
    - Players are updated once per second in 6 slots; an alert is re-sent only when its text changes; sensors in 12 buckets.
