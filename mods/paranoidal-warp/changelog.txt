Version: 9.2.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Major Features:
    - Stage 4 of the warp odyssey: warp years instead of evolution (ПР-077). Every successful jump adds years to the force: home 5-10, BnL Coverage Zone 10-20, BnL Fleet Necropolis 20-35, Cut-off Zone 30-50. A chronometer "En route: N years" over every bridge (platform hub) once the first jump is made, and in the warp drive window.
    - BnL bills the Comfort field subscription: a ship with a field generator gets a bill for the years of its jump, one good from a list (plastic, advanced circuits, batteries, engines, explosives; from 150 years also processing units, low-density structures, electric engines, robot frames, rocket fuel) times the years, due in 15 minutes. The bridge pays by itself from its storage, worst quality first (defects accepted). Every overdue bill takes 30 % off the field's maximum strength (70 / 40 / 10 %), so in a warp sector the field is weak and demons come; paying restores it. The open bill is shown under the chronometer and in the drive window.
    - Letters from home at 40, 100, 200, 300, 500 and 700 warp years.
  Info:
    - Remote interface paranoidal-warp: years_status(platform index) and expire_bills(platform index) for automated tests.

Version: 9.1.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Major Features:
    - Stage 3 of the warp odyssey (user decisions 2026-11-07). Comfort field generator (a recoloured cryogenic plant, one per platform, platform only, 5 MW): its hidden recipe needs and makes nothing, its craft rate is the field strength; a shimmering dome over the ship in a warp sector, a crack for every 8 % of missing strength; outside the warp sectors it switches itself off.
    - Demons of the four sins of consumption (Sale Rage: fast melee; Fine Print: spitters; Garbage: slow and tough; Eternal Excess: frail, very fast, twice as many), three sizes each, recoloured vanilla biters and spitters without loot. Only in warp sectors and only while the field is weak: pressure grows by (1 - strength) x sector danger (0.6 / 1 / 1.5) per second, a wave every 30; waves grow, bigger demons from the 3rd and 6th wave; at most 40 aboard. They appear on the foundation away from the player, attack the ship's buildings and stay until killed.
    - Void shield (a recoloured accumulator, platform only, 100 MJ, charges at 5 MW, gives nothing back; several add up): an asteroid within 4 tiles of the foundation bursts on it for 25 kJ per health point (medium 10 MJ, big 50 MJ); asteroid hits on buildings are healed for 50 kJ per point. Empty, it lets asteroids through. Chunks pass.
    - Red alert: a red pulsing light over the ship, the silo alarm every 5 s and AUTO messages while demons are aboard, for 30 s after a hull breach (foundation lost without mining) or after an unshielded asteroid hit.
    - Technologies Comfort field (500, after the warp drive) and Void shield (1000, after the field); the drive warns when it jumps into a sector without a field. The warp drive is recoloured violet now, not only its icon.
  Info:
    - Remote interface paranoidal-warp: status(platform index) for automated tests.

Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Major Features:
    - First version (Space Age branch of Paranoidal, user decisions 2026-11-07, stage 2 of the warp odyssey). Three warp sectors as Space Age space locations: BnL Coverage Zone (sun as in home orbit, many chunks, a few small asteroids), BnL Fleet Necropolis (half the sun, metal, medium and big asteroids), Cut-off for Non-payment Zone (no sun, carbonic and oxide asteroids). Each has a gate and two points joined by routes of 12000 km, its own asteroids, sky (another planet behind the platform or none, a colour grade) and sun.
    - Warp drive (built only on a space platform, one per platform): an assembling machine with one fixed recipe, 1 warp charge, 30 s at 10 MW. It starts only on the player's command in its window, from a sector gate or home orbit; the platform waits while it spins up (AUTO countdown), then jumps to the chosen gate or home and the screen flashes. Cancel or leaving the gate stops it; the spin-up progress is kept.
    - Warp charge (made at home, 100 kg, ten per rocket) and the warp drive technology (after the platform thrusters) that unlocks the drive, the charge and every point of the sectors.
    - The platform hub is named the bridge of the Axiom-7 and carries the weathered BnL logo of zzzparanoidal-start-bnl.
  Info:
    - Without Space Age nothing is defined and the control script does nothing. paranoidal-space-age-filter lets this mod see Space Age.
    - Remote interface paranoidal-warp (select_target, jump, problem) does what the window does; for automated tests.
