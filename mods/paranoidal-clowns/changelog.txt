---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Major Features:
    - One mod instead of Clowns-Processing 2.0.14 (MadClown01's Processing), Clowns-Nuclear 2.0.08 (MadClown01's Vanilla Nuclear) and Clowns-AngelBob-Nuclear 2.0.13 (MadClown01's AngelBob Nuclear) by MadClown01 and Pezzawinkle. Item, fluid, recipe, technology, entity, subgroup and locale key names are unchanged, so buildings, items and research of existing saves stay.
  Changes:
    - Each stage runs the former mods in their old order (Nuclear, Processing, AngelBob). The mod loads after paranoidal-petrochem, so the nuclear part now comes after Angel's Smelting, Bioprocessing and Industries instead of right after Angel's Petrochem.
    - Removed the startup settings MCP_enable_centrifuges (always switched off with Bob's Assembly), fluid-cleanup (its Bob's fluid group does not exist in Bob's 2.x) and artillery-shells (on in the build): the nuclear and thermonuclear artillery shells are always there; the Clowns centrifuges mk2/mk3 and their technologies are gone with their setting.
    - Removed: the empty control.lua of Clowns-Nuclear, the Clowns-Processing 1.3.19 migration (an old 1.1 reset that would run again under the new name), the Industrial Revolution 3, Early Nuclear Power, pyrawores, SpaceMod and Bob's bullet branches (mods not in the build), the branches for builds without Angel's Petrochem, commented-out code, todo files, unused and duplicate graphics (the AngelBob part shared 31 pictures byte for byte with the nuclear part) and the German locales.
    - Locales: English and Russian only.
  Graphics:
    - Only the 34 pictures the game uses are kept, plus the magnesium plate icon that only Artisanal Reskins replaces (of 162 graphics files in the three mods); they are recompressed (lossy palette; the sluicer sprites and the tinted thorium salt icon lossless): 0.76 MB -> 0.63 MB of kept graphics, the whole mod 2.2 MB -> 1.0 MB.
  Bugfixes:
    - The thermonuclear bomb no longer gets its module and reactor ingredients twice (the AngelBob part repeated them, the last one with the invalid type "name"); zzzparanoidal no longer has to remove them.
    - The magnesium steel sink molten-steel-smelting-c2 is only created without Bob's plates or the Angel's Industries overhaul, as in 1.1 (the 2.0 port always created it next to molten-iron-smelting-6); zzzparanoidal no longer has to hide it.
    - Nuclear fuel reprocessing asked for the missing item "plutonium-239"; it uses angels-plutonium-239.
    - Global variables of the AngelBob ordering table, the osmium bullet and the nuclear action property list are local now.
  Saves:
    - Nothing is lost: the old mods kept no script state. The hidden recipe molten-steel-smelting-c2 disappears. Values of the removed settings in mod-settings.dat are ignored.
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: recipes use categories (RecipePrototype::category and additional_categories were merged into categories; the first entry keeps the meaning of the former main category, a changed main category keeps the other entries).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: product probability renamed to independent_probability (recipe results, minable results, generated products).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: items use fuel_categories (ItemPrototype::fuel_category was removed).
