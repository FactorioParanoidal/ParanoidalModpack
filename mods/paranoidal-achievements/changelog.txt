---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Bugfixes:
    - The unstuck achievement follows TimedSpawnControl-reforged: both the availability guard and GUI owner check use the renamed mod.
    - Milestone imports and remote presets are normalised before use; malformed entries, non-finite numbers, non-plain tables and non-growing next formulas are rejected. Research levels and their steps must be whole numbers. Provider failures are isolated and removed providers leave no stale local presets.
    - Infinite milestones have a bounded completion budget; deferred backfill continues in later cycles. Research milestones now create subsequent levels too, without duplicate next stages after a manual reset.
    - Fixed the milestone reached custom event and the player index used for per-client file output.
    - Historical milestone estimates include weighted aliases. Shared commands and time editing require admin permissions and reject invalid or future times.
    - Force merging preserves completed milestone times, scripted counters, sets and earned force achievements; pending cargo is reassigned. Incompatible no-crash streaks restart.
    - Sandbox deaths no longer affect the real death counter. Entity-count achievements, wind checks, workshop tracking and logistic-network checks exclude lab progress even without force isolation.
    - Movement trackers use the physical controller instead of the remote-view camera; car-speed polls also exclude sandbox players.
    - Fixed stale editor selection after import, descending multi-row deletion, multiplayer notifications and accumulation of the backfill result across forces.
    - JSON exports no longer rewrite user strings. The editor preserves exact normal quality, alias quality, tooltips and untouched unavailable targets/qualities, and prevents a stale admin draft from overwriting a newer shared set.
    - Stable milestone IDs replace fractional sorting indices as GUI identity; display history is paged and column counts cannot fall below one. Existing GUI state is rebuilt lazily without resetting milestone progress.
    - Achievement entries are staged before applying base changes or reserving names; incompatible extra thresholds are skipped without renumbering later tiers. Explicit defect targets respect ic-more-qualities classification.
    - The two defect-production achievements now target burner inserters and stone furnaces instead of materials that cannot be defective; IDs, qualities and quantities are unchanged.
    - Removed accidental scratch globals and a duplicate sprite declaration. Added /paranoidal-achievements-status for tracking diagnostics.
  Locale:
    - Simplified the EN/RU milestone history notifications: earlier completion times are approximate, without statistics-backfill implementation jargon.
    - The "Crane Operator" achievement speaks of loading cranes (ru: погрузочных кранов), as the cranes and their technologies are now named.
    - ru: the "Old-School Dispatcher" achievement names the station controller instead of the Cybersyn combinator.
  Major Features:
    - One mod instead of MxlChievements-Reforged 9.1.0 (achievements: MxlChievements by Mxlppxl, More Achievements by LuziferSenpai and the Paranoidal content) and Milestones 9.4.8 (Paranoidal fork of Milestones 1.4.7 by Wiwiweb). Achievement, setting, hotkey, shortcut, sprite, style, command and locale key names are unchanged; the remote interface "milestones" keeps its name.
  Optimizations:
    - Thirteen production achievement series share their existing tier-1 PNG across all three tiers; electric-furnace tiers 2 and 3 share the tier-2 PNG while tier 1 keeps its distinct picture. Removed 27 byte-identical PNG duplicates. Achievement IDs, conditions and appearance are unchanged.
    - Every event is registered once for the milestones and the achievements together; one on_tick handler replaces the on_tick of Milestones, the on_nth_tick(60) of the achievement polls and the on_nth_tick(240) of the delayed milestone messages.
    - The production statistics are read only by the milestones cycle. The "Chemist" achievement (every Angels gas) asks that cycle for the gases it has not seen yet, at most once per 10 minutes, instead of reading every surface itself.
    - One Blueprint Sandboxes check (sandbox forces and lab surfaces) for both parts.
    - Graphics recompressed: 131 of 134 PNG pixel-identical and smaller, the thumbnail, the spidertron picture and the milestone type icons lossy (1.49 -> 1.30 MB).
  Changes:
    - The setting "Ticks between each check" (milestones_check_frequency) also sets how often the statistics are read for the achievements; its description says so.
    - The Milestones version migrations are removed: the storage of the new mod name is always created by on_init.
    - Locales: English and Russian only (the other Milestones translations covered only the milestone window).
    - Saves: the storage of the two old mods cannot move to a new mod name and is lost (agreed). Scripted achievement counters start again; milestones are found again on the first load (items already made get an estimated time from the production graphs, research times are unknown, manual edits of the milestone list and times are lost).
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
