# Paranoidal Alien Biomes

Mod ID: `Paranoidal-Alien-biomes` · Version: **9.8.6**

Alien Biomes and its graphics, combined with shared tint bases for Paranoidal.
Formerly Alien-biomes-Reforged; the old and new mods cannot be enabled together.
User-optimised graphics are preserved. Prototype names, setting keys and the
`alien-biomes-reforged` remote interface are unchanged. No rename migrations were added;
the existing upstream migration files are untouched.

## Natural habitats and field exploration

New Nauvis maps gain additive, climate-sensitive natural details built from existing
artwork: damp lowlands with faintly glowing root colonies, wet and dry botanical
margins, broken surface-silt traces, mineral outcrops and layered crater formations.
Existing terrain, tree placement and resource-generation weights are not replaced.
Outcrops indicate favourable geology, not guaranteed ore deposits; mining them or
impact breccia yields ordinary stone. Root colonies yield ordinary wood.

An optional **field notebook** in the shortcut bar records nearby colonies,
outcrops and impact breccia. Observations are personal and manual, with coordinates,
visits, the last observed season and historical notes about nearby clearing or
production. There are no research requirements, rewards or automatic exploration.
Records are bounded to 128 per player and 512 across the save.

Colonies add restrained native light and forest ambience. Optional personal seasonal
sound accents use NightBrightness's calendar on Nauvis; they do not change the
calendar or global night brightness. Economy mode reduces moving lights, not habitat
locations. Existing surface map-generation settings and explored terrain are not
rewritten: use a new map for the intended habitat generation.
