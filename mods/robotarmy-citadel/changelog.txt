---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026.10.26
  Major Features:
    - The replicator citadel: when the biters reach 50 % evolution (map setting), a fortified city of replicator droids appears on Nauvis, 50 chunks beyond the edge of the player base (spawn point + base radius 10 chunks, random direction on land, never on player buildings). One citadel per game, at war with every player force, in truce with the biters.
    - The city grows by one module chunk every 10 minutes, next to the modules it has. Every module is a walled chunk of the "city of robots": floor plating, a road cross that runs on through the neighbours, a plaza with the hub pylon and lamps, corner towers, buildings in four quarters. Walls stand only on the outer edges, with gates where the roads leave; a new neighbour removes the shared wall; inner corners are closed.
    - Module types: the hive (core), seven droid foundries (light, assault, fire support, sniper, energy weapons, aircraft, terminators; each makes its own droids), power plants, bastions, turret nests, mortar batteries, minefields, repair docks, supply depots, wreck recyclers, radars, jammers, relay masts, armour works, weapons laboratories, targeting arrays, computing centres and, from 25 modules, shield amplifiers and shield regenerators. The first modules after the hive come in a fixed order (light foundry, power plant, bastion, assault foundry, radar), then by weighted chance with unlock sizes and limits; the power plants follow the power the modules need.
    - Production: every foundry (and the hive) makes droids by script, one per cycle; the droids gather on the hive plaza and march on the player base as soon as the squad is full (8 droids + 1 per foundry module, at most 40; at most 6 attacking squads; at most 400 droids, beyond that the citadel grows stronger, not more numerous). Extra droids stay as the garrison; a squad defends the city when a player destroys something in it.
    - Intelligence (computing centres, levels 0-6): from half-gathered squads that fight what they meet, to discipline and retreat after heavy losses, target choice (buildings without towers first), memory of losses (avoids the chunks where droids died, also on the route), march without distraction, pincer attacks of two squads, and strikes on the power with damaged squads returning to the repair docks.
    - Radars explore the player base chunk by chunk from its centre (only chunks with buildings are followed); jammers unchart the city from the player maps; relay masts turn the growth towards the player base and extend the jammers.
    - Upgrades from the standing modules: armour works raise the armour mark of new droids (I-III; separate droid prototypes), the armour focus adapts to the damage type that killed most droids; weapons laboratories +10 % damage and targeting arrays +8 % rate of fire for droids and towers (force bonuses on the Robot Army droid ammo categories); supply depots +8 % production speed; recyclers turn lost droids into free ones; repair docks heal droids and buildings.
    - From 25 modules (with Defence Tower Reforged): every module gets a defence tower with shields (the citadel force gets the shield research); shield amplifier and regenerator modules raise the capacity and regeneration research levels of the citadel.
    - From 100 modules the foundries get 10 % faster for every hour (map setting), without a limit.
    - The power plants follow the power the modules need, with 1.5 MW per module for the shields after the shield stage; when the shields of all modules start charging at once the city runs short of power for about a minute.
    - When the citadel appears, the chunks on the way to the player base are generated (the squads cannot find paths through chunks that were never generated).
    - Destroying the hive stops the growth for an hour (the foundries keep working); then the foundry module farthest from the player base becomes the new hive and the old one is rebuilt as a foundry. With the hive down and every foundry destroyed, the citadel is defeated and its remaining buildings blow up.
    - Ruined modules (all key buildings destroyed) give no bonus and are rebuilt before the city grows further; next, a standing module that lost its hub pylon (no power) gets its missing buildings back. Items on the ground (the loot of the destroyed buildings) and the citadel's own droids in the way of a building are moved aside, so the loot stays and the building can stand. A hive that cannot be placed is tried again; a hive destroyed while being built counts as lost.
    - Every module of the shield stage gets the full range of the defence tower (its ten range levels are researched for the citadel force), so one tower covers the whole chunk; the tower also repairs the citadel buildings with repair packs.
    - The citadel clears its surroundings: every one to two minutes it removes a few biter nests and worms within 5 chunks of the city (removed by script: no evolution).
    - Loot: destroyed replicator droids drop parts of their bodies by kind (gears, plates of Bob's metals, bearing balls and bearings, rods, cables, circuit boards, electronic components, circuits), destroyed key buildings drop replicator cores; the core decryption recipe (learned when the citadel appears) takes a core apart into advanced parts.
    - Remote interface robotarmy-citadel: status(), modules(), squads(), spawn(x, y), grow(n, type), defeat() and configure(overrides) (map settings for this save, e.g. growth_ticks, stun_ticks; configure(nil) clears them).
    - Messages: a warning at 45 % evolution, the place of the citadel (map tag and chat link), the shield and acceleration stages, the fall of the hive, a new hive, the defeat. Command /citadel shows its state.
  Compatibility:
    - Kills of replicator droids by Robot Army droids count for the droid ranks (remote function credit_kill of Robot Army Reforged); Robot Army droids attack the citadel like any enemy.
    - Truce (cease-fire both ways) with the "enemy" force and every AI controlled force; RampantFixed ignores forces in cease-fire with the biters.
    - The citadel force is AI controllable (needed to create unit groups by script); it has no unit spawners, so it neither expands nor sends pollution attacks by itself.
  Optimizations:
    - No unit spawners, no on_tick: one heartbeat every 60 ticks with bounded portions of work (a few foundries, one look at each squad every two seconds, a few radar, repair and validation looks), a build queue registered only while something is built (3 buildings per 4 ticks), the death event filtered to the citadel's own droids and buildings. One command per squad, never per droid; marches in segments with low-priority cached paths.
    - A squad is given its next step only when its command is over or it stands still while not fighting, gathering or waiting for a path (so slow path requests are not restarted).
    - Towers, lamps and most buildings need no script: towers run on the citadel power network (destroying the power plants darkens them), lamps have no power, buildings are simple entities with animations.
