  Features:
    - Look of defective buildings: a defective building is always darker than a standard one, brightness 75% / 65% / 55% / 45% / 35% at -1 ... -5, with a warm rust cast growing with the grade (luminance kept), and dark smoke while working: buildings with their own smoke (furnaces, burner drills, boilers, steam engines) add it at their chimney, other machines, labs and generators puff from the top with a chance of grade/5; the smoke is blended from the base smoke into the train smoke (50 / 70 / 85 / 94%), -5 is an exact copy of train-smoke; 1-3 smokes per look, one look per building every 20 ticks. Standard and positive quality are unchanged.
    - Look of defective buildings: no soot stains (they stayed as a static dark spot next to an idle building, user observation 2026-11-04). A save with the stains redraws its defective buildings once without them.
    - At data load every building whose item can be defective gets a sprite per direction from the first frame of its body graphics (no shadows, lights, glows, masks, ground integration); control draws it over the building with a premultiplied tint (render objects attached to the building, destroyed with it; drawn on build, robot build, script build/revive, clone, rotation, flip and production mode switch; all redrawn at a configuration change). Buildings whose layers overlap use only the layers of prototypes/defect-look-layers.lua, generated from the layer pixels outside the mod, so that no pixel is darkened twice. Radars, walls, gates, rocket silos, vehicles and units have no silhouette. 110 buildings, 179 sprites in the Paranoidal build. Temporary map setting "Defect look: darkening layer" (building layer / above buildings) for the visual check. Engine check test-runs/ic-look-smoke3-20261104: silhouettes per direction, mode twin, brightness and rust cast per grade, smoke prototypes (-5 equals train-smoke), no look for standard and positive quality, objects destroyed with the building, working defective furnace and burner drill with the smoke list.
    - Production mode window: the rows "Operation time" and "Power" show the real values of the opened machine in every mode instead of x1 / x5 / x20: the time of one operation of the current recipe (a furnace without a recipe: the last one) at the machine speed with its quality, modules and beacons, and the power drawn while working with its quality, modules and beacons, drain included (consumption bonus kept by the engine at -80% or more). Without a recipe the rows keep the multipliers; every cell has its multiplier in the tooltip. Updated every 10 ticks while the window is open, like the quality chance. Engine checks test-runs/ic-power1-20261102 and ic-panel1-20261102: measured operation time and draw of assembling machines in modes I-III, of several qualities, a defect grade, speed and efficiency modules equal the shown values.
    - Remote interface "ic-more-qualities", get_counters(force_index): the counters of the quality-control panel (refining workshop attempts and so on). Used by the base journal of paranoidal-CodexRPG.
    - Registry of recipes whose result is always normal: mod-data "ic-normal-result" (data.lua), filled by other mods until data-final-fixes. The engine gives the result of a recipe with allow_quality = false the quality of its ingredients, and a player sets that quality by circuit, blueprint or pasted settings; for these recipes quality modules are forbidden and the defect scheduler and handcraft reset a positive result to normal. Used for the Yuoki currencies and the copying recipes of zzzparanoidal.
    - Registry of crafting machines that never take quality modules: mod-data "ic-quality-closed" (data.lua, exists with quality), filled by other mods until data-final-fixes. "Quality everywhere" does not add the quality effect to these machines; data-final-fixes drops unknown names and logs the list. Used by zzzparanoidal for the bio industry, Angels electrolysers and filtration units.
    - Fuel acceleration and top speed bonus per quality level is capped so that the highest quality gives at most x8 of the normal fuel multiplier.
    - Chest and storage slots (and cargo wagons that take quality): inventory size multiplier of positive qualities grows linearly with the level up to x8 at the highest quality. In old saves the engine shrinks quality chests on load; items that do not fit are lost.
    - Control post: new requirement "Post-processing to" a quality. Order products below the quality stay in the post; every 5 of one quality become 1 product that goes up a tier with 600% of the next tier probability (at most 100%, further tiers with their own probability, like the engine roll), one operation every 15 ticks, no power or modules. Accepted products go forward, defects left, the rest right after the order is complete. Materials are included; items with contents, data or spoil timers are not taken. When the post is full of single copies of many tiers, the 5 lowest are combined. The post window explains the rule with the numbers of the build and shows the waiting count, operations, items used and steps up; new signal "Order: waiting for post-processing". New mock test tests/expansion-process.lua.
    - Control post: the output arrow on the building is the yellow indication arrow of the game (like inserters and drills) instead of the green one, always visible; older posts get it when they are looked at or their window is opened. The window has no output row with rotate buttons any more (the rotate key on the building turns it); the circuit and Repeat checkboxes are in one column at the bottom.
    - Control post window: the estimate "Next finished product ~ %" (always 0% on high goals) is replaced by "Best inside: [quality] (N) of goal" with a bar: the highest tier the post has reached on the way to the goal.

    - Control post window redesigned: a status strip under the title like the game windows (LED, short state, details in the tooltip) with three colored icon buttons on the right (green play: start, resume after a pause, new batch; blue pause, pressed while paused; red stop), unavailable ones disabled; the order as a recipe "[standard product] 5 to 1 -> [product at the goal quality] x quantity"; the expected cost of one finished product (products and artifacts) before the start; "Accepted N / M" and an estimate "Next finished product ~ %" from the improved products inside; one line "in processing, used, artifacts"; output, Repeat and circuit control at the bottom; an empty-state hint without an order.

Before, it kept using products and artifacts while the output was blocked. The window says whether the output is full or there is nothing in front of the arrow.
    - Control post window reworked after player feedback: title "Post-processing"; secondary counters (operations, steps up, finished products sent, batches, artifacts used) in the tooltip of an info icon on the right of the title; a full-width "Accepted N / M" bar; products used shown; rows spaced; the quantity is applied by Enter or a green confirm button instead of every keystroke (each change restarted the count); "Chances" text button; output side buttons on the right; a bottom row with "Repeat" on the left and Start / Pause / Cancel on the right, unavailable actions disabled (also after circuit commands). Info icons of the other relative panels moved to the right of their title.
    - Control post runs on small alien artifacts (bob-small-alien-artifact): one artifact pays for 4 post-processing operations, set by the new map setting "Control post: operations per small alien artifact" (1-1000). Without artifacts the post waits and takes nothing. The last slot of the input row is filtered to the artifact. The window shows the artifacts inside, the operations paid and the artifacts used, warns in red without them; the chances window has a column of artifacts per accepted product. Without the item in the game the post works for free.
    - Control post slots: 50 instead of 8, each with its own filter: a row of 10 input slots for standard copies, one slot per tier below the goal, the rest for the goal and better. Before, inserters filled every slot with standard copies and the improved copies were dropped on the ground; now an operation whose result has no slot waits (the window names the tier) and nothing is ever dropped. The fallback "combine the 5 lowest of a full post" is removed. Items with a stack size below 5 are not taken.
    - Control post does only post-processing (user decision 2026-10-31): the requirements "strictly standard" and "at least", the refinement and "other" outputs and their legend are removed. The post has one output at its arrow for accepted products; its slots are filtered to the order item at standard or better (inventory type "with_filters_and_bar"), so other items cannot be put in; strays, defects and leftovers below the order stay inside and the window asks to take them out. An order needs a quality above standard and an item the post upgrades. The window: item, "upgrade to" quality with tier numbers, quantity, buttons, live counters; the "i" button opens a separate window with the goal, the rule and a table of the step-up chance and the average number of products per accepted product for every quality below the goal. Older posts and blueprints: "at least X" becomes "up to X", "strictly standard" an empty order. The signal "Order: waiting for refinement" stays but is no longer sent.

  Optimizations:
    - A machine in a production mode needs no scheduler looks after quality control 10 and one per operation before it (was a look every few ticks to rewind its progress, about 110 per flawless operation), plus one beacon check every 1.5 s.
    - Refinement workshop: looked at every 30 ticks while an attempt runs (more often while its progress is slowed by a mode or low power, so the furnace bar runs at most 3% ahead), one tick before the engine can complete an attempt and every 30 ticks while idle (was every 3 and 6 ticks); its inventories and full power are kept on the record. An item put into an idle workshop waits up to half a second before the workshop takes it; circuit commands and returning power are seen within half a second.
    - Control post: an idle, paused or finished post without circuit control and without items is not looked at until start, pause, cancel, a new order or new settings wake it; an active empty post looks every 10 ticks (was 5).
    - Dead code removed: draw_unless_upgraded, eligibility.is_finished, modes.definition, stats.reset, rules.prepay, workshop mining fix-up, unused locale keys.
    - The defect scheduler watches only what can need handling: crafting machines whose recipes can make a defect-capable product, take a finished ingredient or recycle (found at load from the recipes; in Paranoidal 55 of 336 machine prototypes: assemblers, Bob electronics machines, chemical plants, farms, droid assemblers, recyclers), machines with a production mode and defective drills. Furnaces, refineries, Angels ore processing and standard or positive drills are never tracked.
    - One look per craft that needs handling (decision at the first look, the next look right after the end) instead of two; a machine whose recipe needs nothing sleeps (a look every 5 seconds) and is woken by its open window (every 10 ticks), pasted settings, a production mode or a lost quality-control level 10. Assemblers whose recipe is set by the circuit network keep short looks.
    - Defective drills are slowed by rewinding the engine progress ahead of time (about 1/m + 1 looks per cycle instead of 8-12); the progress bar of such a drill fills and restarts a few times per cycle.
    - Production modes: a slowed craft is looked at one full-speed tick before the engine could complete it (fewer looks); machine power is kept per record.
    - The scheduler, the refinement workshop and the control post share a timer wheel: no table is walked on ticks without work (before, every workshop and post every tick). A control post whose outputs take nothing looks again after 4 ticks instead of every tick; the last look of a workshop before a completion comes one tick before it instead of every tick; full workshop power is kept per record.
    - Prototype data (recipe products and ingredients, categories, machine relevance, drill speed and mining time) is compiled once per load; quality grades are table lookups instead of string matching; defect distributions are built once; handcraft reads the cached quality-control level and the crafting queue only when it is not empty.
    - Older saves: the scheduler storage is rebuilt by the new rules on first access (running crafts are seen anew), workshop and post records are scheduled on the next tick, panel counters of losses and operations are removed.
    - After quality control 10 standard and positive drills are not tracked at all (they were looked at every 10 seconds); a lost research 10 finds them again on every surface. Handcrafted standard or positive items are not processed after quality control 10 (no crafting queue scan).
    - After quality control 10 the defect scheduler almost stops: standard and positive drills, and machines without a production mode and without a defective recipe are looked at every 10 seconds instead of several times per operation. Defective drills, moded machines and crafts from defective ingredients (capped result, recycling to standard) are handled as before. A defective craft that starts and ends between two rare looks is not handled. A lost research 10 brings every machine back to normal looks within a second.
    - The quality-control level of a force is kept and refreshed on research events and every 10 seconds instead of being counted on every scheduler tick.
    - Settings are cached (startup: until a configuration change; map: until a setting change) and the scheduler context is built once instead of on every tick.
    - A machine is decided with one look 2-3 ticks before the operation ends instead of approaching it in several looks (drills too). A sudden speed-up between looks (modules, beacons) can let an operation complete unobserved.
    - A machine waiting with a set recipe is looked at after half of that recipe, not of the fastest recipe of its category.
    - Drills: mining speed, mining time and energy per prototype are cached; the target resource is compared directly, without a string key; a depleted drill is looked at every 5 seconds.
    - Refinement workshop: looked at every 3 ticks while working (more often for very short attempts) and every 6 ticks while idle instead of every 1-2 ticks; the pack resource is counted only for the circuit signal and the panel.
    - Control post: the building in front of each output is remembered (re-checked every 5 seconds, a missing one searched again after half a second); an active post without items looks every 5 ticks instead of every tick.
    - Production mode window: the modes are numbered I. Normal, II. Precise, III. Flawless (Russian: Обычный, Точный, Эталонный; mode 3 was "Maximum"), the table header is I / II / III and the column of the selected mode is highlighted. Short new mode tooltips without losses and "independent rolls"; the grey note under the table is replaced by a native info icon next to the title with a short explanation. Before quality control 10 a "Defect chance" row is added. The quality-module chance follows modules and beacons while the window is open (every 10 ticks, only the opened machine); a changed control level rebuilds the window.
    - Quality-control panel: mode names follow the mode window (precise / flawless); loss rows and the losses column are shown only when losses are enabled in the map settings (off by default).
    - Refinement workshop modes: I. Normal (as before), II. Precise (5 times the attempt time, the same repair packs and power, +2 grades for sure), III. Flawless (20 times the time, the same packs, standard quality for sure); never above standard. Selected in the "Refinement" panel with a table like the production mode window; applies from the next attempt; copied with the other workshop settings (copy-paste, blueprints, replacement, rebuilt ghosts). Older workshops get mode I. Mode attempts are counted separately in the refinement statistics and do not enter the 90% success check.
    - The production mode and refinement panels can be collapsed with a close button in their title into a blue square button with the icon of the selected mode (standard quality icon, hourglass, crosshair); clicking it expands the panel. Expanded by default; the state is kept per player, separately for machines and for the workshop.

    - Production modes: the extra quality-module rolls are made shortly before the operation completes (with the modules installed then), not at its start. An operation that completes unobserved after a sharp speed-up gets none.
  Bugfixes:
    - Handcraft: a manually queued batch no longer skips defects and losses because a recipe using the item is queued after it (e.g. 50 inserters + 1 fast inserter). Only the prerequisites the game queued itself are protected; at most the last item of a manual batch can still be.
    - Production modes: a machine stopped during a moded operation (circuit, no power) no longer pays the extra power; it is looked at every 15 ticks, and the progress made before the first look after it resumes is not counted (it was not paid for).
    - Production modes: switching a machine to normal (also by Shift+click or a blueprint) during a precise/maximum operation no longer stops its extra power. The operation still finishes in its mode, so it now keeps paying for it; the mode record is dropped when that operation ends.
    - Production modes, burner machines: the extra energy is burnt from the fuel slot as well, not only from the item that is burning (long checks between looks undercharged it, mostly in maximum mode). Burner effectivity is applied; missing fuel slows the operation like missing electricity.
    - Production modes: the extra power is released when a machine runs a normal operation, a recipe too fast to be handled, or waits for owed ingredients.
