Version: 1.1.1
Date: 2026. 10. 12
  Changes:
    - Refinement workshop makes one attempt per item: the same item always leaves, improved (90% +1 grade, 10% for every further grade, never above standard) or unchanged (10%). No repeated attempts inside the machine.
    - Armor, vehicles (item-with-entity-data) and items with inventory or tags are not accepted by the workshop: a recipe would recreate them without their contents.
    - The side panel of the workshop window shows only the two settings (reset positive quality, circuit signals); progress bars, outcome table and notes are removed. Shorter workshop and input slot descriptions.
    - Workshop and control post are built from the burner assembling machine and unlocked by burner mechanics (fallback: assembling machine 1 and quality control 1).
    - Recycling keeps native quality again: results are at least the quality of the recycled item and quality modules can raise it (all recycler tiers). Recycling recipes are no longer forced white and get no defect draw; defective results become standard.
    - Epic and legendary quality researches without quality unlocks are hidden (they only carried a note effect).
    - Paranoidal defaults: initial loss chance 0% (losses disabled), finished products protected from losses.
    - Production modes renamed to normal / precise / maximum; power x5 and x20 (energy per operation x25 and x400), speed unchanged. Mode table: numbered columns, numeric worst grade, no loss row.
    - New startup setting "quality for all production" (off by default): quality modules fit every assembler, furnace and drill with module slots; raw resources and intermediates can get positive quality; defects stay on finished products.
    - Hidden production-mode power consumers have a name (was an unknown locale key in the electric network window).

Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026. 10. 10
  Features:
    - Paranoidal refinement workshop: repairs defective items one grade at a time with any repair packs (durability is drained gradually; partially used packs count). 90% success, 10% chance for every further grade; cost depends only on the item (science kinds x square root of health).
    - The workshop can reset positive quality to standard (optional, guaranteed, no packs, 1/5 time); standard items pass for free.
    - Production modes for assembling machines and furnaces: careful (x5 time, x10 energy, losses /10, defects as +4 control levels, quality modules as 3 rolls) and precise (x20 time, x100 energy, no losses, no defects, quality modules as 10 rolls).
    - Control post: production orders (item, strictly standard / at least a quality, quantity, repeat) with three rotatable outputs: accepted, to refinement, other.
    - Circuit signals and commands for workshop and post; quality-control panel (Ctrl+Shift+Q) with production, refinement and batch tabs.
    - Settings of workshops, posts and modes follow copy-paste, blueprints, fast replace and rebuilt ghosts.
  Changes:
    - Mod folder renamed to ic-more-qualities (no version in the name).
    - Workshop and post are unlocked by quality control 1.
    - Event handlers use one shared dispatcher; outdated defect tests updated (33% default loss, current interfaces).
