---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-01
  Major Features:
    - One mod instead of LargerLamps-2_0 0.2.3 (Larger Lamps 2.0 by Deadlock989, Goakiller900, MasterBuilder, NullHarp), InlaidLampsExtended 2.0.0 (Inlaid Lamps Extended by Mathematician, CobaltTiger, Pi-C), Lighted-Poles-Plus 2.0.4 (Lighted Electric Poles + by Optera) and BigLight 0.3.0 (BIG LIGHT by Ritn). Entity, item, recipe, technology, recipe category, kept setting and locale key names are unchanged, so buildings, recipes and research of existing saves stay.
  Optimizations:
    - Lighted poles: the pole is registered with register_on_object_destroyed and its hidden lamp is kept in storage; the lamp is removed when the pole disappears in any way (mined, killed, upgraded, destroyed by a script without an event) instead of four removal events with a search at the pole position. Dolly moves teleport the stored lamp without a search.
    - Lighted poles: the pole/lamp name lookup is cached per load; no global functions.
    - Train stop lights are built once with their final 2.0 geometry; zzzparanoidal no longer rebuilds them, and the copper lamp no longer carries the 1.1 "hr_version" sprites that zzzparanoidal had to promote.
    - No load-time logging of whole prototype tables (Inlaid Lamps logged the vanilla lamp, both inlaid lamps, both recipes and all collision layers on every start).
    - Graphics recompressed (lossy palette for sprites and icons, lossless for light and tinted glow layers): about 0.74 MB -> 0.37 MB; the copper lamp and the electric copper lamp share one set of pictures (they were byte-identical copies).
  Changes:
    - Removed the startup settings ritnmods-bl-05 (rail signal lights; its code was disabled), lepp_light_max_size (75), lepp_pole_whitelist and lepp_pole_blacklist (none of the listed poles exist in the build), lepp_tech_blacklist (empty), lepp_tech_fallback ("lamp"), lepp_tech_pole_weight ("realistic", 40 kg), inlaid_lamps_extended_change_energy_usage (off) and the inlaid lamp light intensity, coloured light and glow settings (0.9, 1, 6, 0.235, 6). Their values of the build are constants now. Kept: ritnmods-bl-01..04, lepp_light_size_factor, inlaid-lamps-extended-light_size.
    - Removed: the dead control.lua of Larger Lamps (it waited for the mod "DeadlockLargerLamp" and used the 1.1 API), its AAI recipe branch (it checked the misspelled "aai-industries" and never ran) and the unused "aai-production" recipe category it created, the Krastorio 2, Cursed Filter Mining Drill, Hiladdar Mining, ritnmods, Train Stop Nostalgia, LTN, train-scaling, Dectorio, Schall Lamp Contrast, PickerExtended, ShinyBob and nullius branches (mods not in the build), the BIG LIGHT rail signal and "addon-entity" code and graphics, LR sprites, backups and editor files.
    - Recipes of all four lamps are in the "crafting" category (the fallback "basic-crafting" was fixed by zzzparanoidal before); the copper lamp upgrades to the large lamp (was set by zzzparanoidal).
    - Mining drill status lights are found by their vanilla sprite instead of a fixed layer index; Bob's drills switched off by their settings no longer break loading.
    - Lighted poles of a pole whose next upgrade gets no lighted copy no longer point to a missing prototype; the fallback technology always gets an effects list.
    - Lighted poles: researched lighted pole recipes are re-enabled at configuration change (only these recipes, not every recipe of every researched technology as before).
    - The signal colours of the vanilla lamp are copied in data-final-fixes to all five electric lamps (before: the large lamp and the two inlaid lamps).
    - Locales: English and Russian only.
  Bugfixes:
    - Lighted poles: a mod added to a save with lighted poles placed their hidden lamps only at configuration change; on_init now places them too. Cloned lighted poles get a lamp and cloned hidden lamps are removed.
    - Lighted poles: the fallback technology received a single effect table instead of a list when it had no effects.
  Saves:
    - Nothing is lost: the old mods kept no state that matters (Lighted Electric Poles + stored only name lists). At the first load the hidden lamps of all lighted poles are rebuilt. Values of the removed settings in mod-settings.dat are ignored.
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: recipes use categories (RecipePrototype::category and additional_categories were merged into categories; the first entry keeps the meaning of the former main category, a changed main category keeps the other entries).
