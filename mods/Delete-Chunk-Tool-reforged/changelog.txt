Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-10-31
  Major Features:
    - Renamed to Delete-Chunk-Tool-reforged (Paranoidal fork). The item and shortcut keep the name "chunk-eraser"; the old mod kept nothing in its storage, so nothing is migrated.
    - Preview and confirmation: the selected chunks are highlighted on the map and in the world (green - deleted, orange - deleted with buildings or ruins, red - kept) and a dialog lists what is deleted and what is kept; nothing is deleted before "Delete". One summary message instead of an error line for every chunk.
    - Map trimming: shortcut "Trim the map" and the command /chunk-trim [distance] find every chunk of a planet surface farther than the "dct-trim-distance" setting (5 chunks by default) from buildings and characters of player forces, outside the charting range of radars, not visible to any player force and without pollution; they are deleted after the same preview and confirmation and are uncharted.
    - Regenerated chunks: every deleted chunk is remembered. When it is generated again, its ore is cut down to the amount it had before deletion (mined ore does not grow back, mined-out ore does not come back) and AbandonedRuins_updated_fork, RSO-reforged and AbandonedRuins-paranoidal spawn no ruins, old mines or legendary ruins there (remote "is_regenerated"). Chunks deleted with the tool come back without enemy spawners, worms and units (destroyed with script_raised_destroy); trimmed chunks keep the enemies of the map generator.
  Changes:
    - A normal selection also keeps chunks with ruins (neutral buildings, items on the ground, ruin forces) and legendary ruins of AbandonedRuins-paranoidal; an alternative selection (Shift) deletes them and warns that legendary ruins are lost for good. Chunks with a character are never deleted, in any mode.
    - Remote "count_trimmed": MxlChievements-Reforged counts only chunks deleted with the tool for "World Eraser".
    - Deleted chunks are uncharted for every force.
    - Single player: the tool works without the administrator flag.
    - Russian locale.
  Graphics:
    - The icon is renamed to graphics/chunk-eraser.png; it and thumbnail.png are recompressed (pingo -s4 -quality=45, lossy, same dimensions): 59.9 -> 33.5 KB, worst PSNR 34.4 dB.
  Optimizations:
    - Chunks are checked by entity counts with a limit instead of building entity lists; classification and deletion run a batch per tick (128 checked, 64 deleted), so large areas do not freeze the game; on_tick is registered only while a job runs.
    - Removed prototype fields that Factorio 2.0 does not read (stackable, show_in_library, toggleable).
  Bugfixes:
    - Map trimming no longer crashes with "Invalid SurfaceIdentification" in on_tick: LuaForce.is_chunk_visible takes the surface first, then the chunk position.
