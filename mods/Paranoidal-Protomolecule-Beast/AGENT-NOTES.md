# Paranoidal-Protomolecule-Beast — notes for agents

Working notes for whoever changes this mod. The player-facing description is `info.json` and `locale/`; the history is `changelog.txt`. Last updated 2026-11-04 (audit: creature sprites rebuilt, sprite check, runtime guards), version 9.0.0, Factorio 2.0.77 (Quality and Elevated Rails, no Space Age).

## Identity

- Paranoidal fork of *The Beast* 2.1.0 by Klaudia (upstream copy: `NEW MOD/The_Beast_2.1.0`, audit in its `_audit/`). Mod name `Paranoidal-Protomolecule-Beast` by user decision of 2026-11-03; it was `The_Beast` before it was in any build or save.
- **Internal names stay** for saves and other mods' calls: prototypes `beast-*`, settings `the-beast-*`, force `the-beast`, remote interface `the_beast`, command `/beast-arrive`, mod-data `the-beast-compounds`. Renaming any of them needs a save assessment and a user decision.
- Lore: the invader is the protomolecule from *The Expanse*, in a grim, clinical voice. The lore base is local agent memory in `agent-context/lore-protomolecule/` (workspace root), **never in this folder**. Read it only for player-visible texts, names or graphics (AGENTS.md route row "The Beast mod"). User decisions on behaviour are in its `adaptation.md`, decision log.
- Location: `ParanoidalModpack-master/mods/Paranoidal-Protomolecule-Beast` since 3622400eb9 (2026-11-04). Nothing here depends on the folder. Still missing from the build rules: its `[mod-name]`/`[mod-description]` in `ParanoidalLocale` and `mods-metadata/!descriptions.json` (the Russian title needs the user's decision).

## Data stage

- A building becomes a creature by its type (`shared.group_for_type`), its own picture (`body_sprite` in `beasts.lua`) and its size: up to `shared.max_lifted_size` (5) tiles; at 4–5 tiles two more legs; rolling stock at any size. Base game buildings (`shared.compounds[].sources`) get a creature per inserter type, others one set of legs plus a walled one. A building with no readable picture is logged and is never lifted.
- Never infected (`shared.is_excluded`): Factorissimo, `pd-relic-*`, `bnl-distress-beacon`, `ic-control-post`, `roboport-interface-*`, `bi-arboretum-area`, `*-rampant`. Aliases (`shared.alias_of`): HeroTurretRedux ranks are lifted as their base turret; lit poles as `small-lamp`, with the pole as a second sample.
- Result for the control stage: mod-data `the-beast-compounds` = `sources[building] = {group, legs, as, samples?}`, `creatures[name] = {group, source, legs}`.
- **Sprites (rewritten 2026-11-04).** A torso is a RotatedAnimation; the building's sprites are Sprites, Animations, RotatedSprites, 4-way sets and variations, and the engine lays out frames differently per type, so nothing is copied across as it is. `north()` picks one sprite out of any set; `leaves()` flattens layers; then per creature kind (`body_of`): still bodies use `still_layer` (one file, the first frame's corner; frames only from one plain grid: a plain `filename` grid, the first stripe of an Animation, the frames 1..m a `frame_sequence` shows when it shows all of them; `filenames` give their first file's first frame only; RotatedSprite `frames` give the first cell's own rectangle) and `breathe` (at most 128 frames: an animation is at most 255 shown frames); car bodies and turret heads use `rotated_animation_layer` (the source's own RotatedAnimation, unchanged, no breathing); rolling stock uses `rotated_sprite_layer` (RotatedSprite → RotatedAnimation with `slice` = `line_length`, `frame_count` 1, `back_equals_front` emulated by listing the files twice when they hold exactly the half circle). Engine layouts the checker relies on, confirmed from vanilla data: RotatedAnimation `filenames` frames run direction by direction, `slice` per row, `lines_per_file` rows per file (small worm: F 10, slice 4, 10 files; small spitter: F 14, slice 6, 3 files); RotatedAnimation `stripes` are a cursor over frames then directions, and every cell of a stripe is read (car, tank; the Wind-Turbines-Reforged error of 2026-11-04 was row 2 of a 5-row stripe of a 2-row file).
- **Sprite check.** Headless runs never load sprites, so a broken rectangle only shows at the graphical start. `review/beast-audit-20261104/_tools/beast_sprites.py DUMP ENGINE_DATA MODS_DIR` checks every frame of every prototype of this mod against the PNG sizes (icons too); `mock_export.lua` runs the data stage in Lua 5.1 against `make_raw2.py` output for a quick check without the engine. On 2026-11-04: 0 problems of 97 418 rectangles (engine dump with creative-mod); it finds both errors of the user's logs on 3622400 and 190 more (rolling stock without `slice`) on 509ba1499e.
- Artillery turret → `howitzer` (base, cannon base and barrel in their first direction, without dice); car/tank → `crawler` (`vehicle = true`: keeps every direction of the car's own animation and turns, no walled version, stays a creature at the lair); artillery wagon → `doom-wagon`. Spidertrons get no creature. `beast-spore-shell` is the base `artillery-projectile` tinted, its action a script trigger `beast-spore-hit`.
- On the build (dump 2026-11-04, creative-mod on): 1284 creature prototypes, 55 legs; bi-dart-turret and creative-mod's two energy voids have no picture.

## Control stage

- **storage**: `infection` (records by unit number, active/dormant lists, markers), `units` (creature records, gatherings, bands, roboports), `outbreak` (one table, below), `arrival`, `the_beast` (setting overrides), train state. A creature record may hold `cargo = {place, quality, owner, samples, contents}` (what it carries home), `carried` (the same, kept by a carriage or vehicle creature that stays one) and `rode` (the raid by train it came on). `contents` is a pack of `contents.lua` with script inventories: every pack is restored, spilled or discarded exactly once, so whatever hands a cargo on clears it from the old record.
- **storage.outbreak**: `phase` (landing → founding → lair → over), `surface`, `landing`, `site`, `core`, `zones`, `tier`, `progress`, `effects`, `catalogue[name] = {tick, quality, analysed}`, `queue`, `analysing`, `buildings[unit_number] = {entity, owner, clone, name, type, rating}` with the sums kept up as they come and go (`power`, `capacity`, `count`, `drills`, `machines`, `guards`, `armed`, `copies[name]`; removed by `on_object_destroyed`, recounted every 5 min and on configuration change), `energy`, `mass`, `raid`, `raids`, `ruin_raids`, `ruin_ok[unit_number]`, `next_raid`, `next_clone`, `clones_made`, `templates` (cached clone lists), `spore_hits`; from lair_arms `artillery[unit_number]`, `spiders[unit_number]`, `shells_fired`; `shell_targets` (lair_arms' shared search); from lair_rails `rails` = `{phase (nil → planning → building → joining → done | failed), planning = {start, specs, placed, tip}, specs[] = {name, position, direction} (index 1 the junction, the last the platform's end), entities[index], own[unit_number], start, junction, last, heading, end_direction, platform (tiles of straight at the end), next_index, blocked, tries, bad[unit_number], joining_since, joined, station, lured, depot[] = {name, quality, owner, color, health, contents}, raider = {raid, carriages, units[unit_number], passengers[], phase (out → waiting → boarding → back), stop, stop_name, position, since, leave_at, lost_since}, rides, taken, captured, routed[train_id], no_route[train_id], lost}`. A raid by train has `raid.ride = {stop, landed}`.
- **Hooks** of outbreak for the libraries on top: lists `on_update`, `on_end`, `on_raid_end`, `status_hooks`, `hurry_hooks`; single functions `on_building(building, sign)` (lair_arms), `on_tier`, `put_down`, `spawn_unit`, `board`, `disembark`, `take_apart` (units), `ride`, `can_board`, `plan_ride`, `board_ride`, `store_carriage` (lair_rails). Exported for them: `state`, `score`, `liftable_types`, `start_raid`, `open_zone`, `place_at_lair`, `spend_mass`, `spend_energy`, `ammo_of`. They require outbreak, never the other way round, and never `require` at runtime (Factorio loads files only while control.lua is parsed).
- **Zones**: infection spreads only inside open zones (the landing site; a raid's target while it lasts; a spore hit). A zone over rails gives the sample `straight-rail`, over a train stop the sample `train-stop`. `infection.infect(entity, true)` (landing, bite, remote) ignores zones. Without an outbreak everything spreads as in the original mod.
- **Repelled** (user decision 2026-11-04, option Б): before a core forms (phases landing and founding), an outbreak with no creature and no infected building ends at once (`outbreak.lua` `update`, `infection.any()`): with `[the-beast] repelled` if it never had a creature (`state.had_creatures`, checked every second), otherwise `extinct`; in the founding also with no creature and nothing infected that can still give one (`infection.can_breed()`: an inserter with a pending `convert_tick`, a roboport) — `no-carriers` after `barren_grace` (1 minute, so that turrets still firing cure the rest first and it ends as repelled, without defects; agent's extension of the same decision, 2026-11-04: turrets out of ammunition left infected drills that could never found a lair); `state.ended` (status `ended`) keeps the key. Typical cause: the player's turrets shoot off the infection markers (`beast-infection`). A cured building is reinfected only from an infected neighbour and only inside an open zone (`check_recovering`): during the landing a few turrets and several infected drills shoot and reinfect each other until the landing zone closes, then the rest is cleared and it ends; many turrets clear everything at once (a user's 2.1 save, 54 gun turrets: 3 markers, nothing left). The landing still picks the outermost outpost whatever defends it.
- **Power**: core 1 MW; each stolen power building in the lair adds its rated power (`get_max_energy_production`, boilers and reactors `get_max_energy_usage`), without fuel. Research speed `1 + 5·extra/(extra + 20 MW)`, at most 6×; a stage takes `research-minutes` (30) at 1×. The energy store (600 MJ + 100× each accumulator's buffer) pays for clones and electric turrets.
- **Idle lair**: every lair building but ammo and electric turrets is `disabled_by_script` with the custom status `the-beast.assimilated` (set in `add_building` and `recount`, cleared in `give_back`): power, mass and analysis are reckoned, not run, so a stolen reactor with fuel doesn't heat up (no meltdown in the lair), furnaces don't smelt, radars don't chart, and 400 buildings cost next to no UPS. Roboports ignore the write (engine rule). Artillery stays fired by script.
- **Mass**: 1/30 per second from the core, 1/10 per stolen drill in the lair, plus the area of every spare copy (beyond 2 of a name; never power) taken apart on arrival.
- **Cloning**: every 10 s from analysed samples of types allowed by the stage, cost = area mass + area·20 MJ; turrets first (4 + 2·stage), then half the time; at most `lair-max-buildings` (400), of them at most 60 turrets (`guards_cap`) and 8 artillery turrets (`artillery_cap`), as idle turrets by the hundred cost UPS for nothing. Turrets are fed 60 at a time each clone tick. Raids on the base clone `raid-clones` turrets beside the target, which dissolve with the raid. Not cloned: rolling stock, flamethrower turrets, vehicles, and anything without a creature (`shared.source_info`). Turrets get ammunition (cheapest ammo of their categories by item order) for mass and electric buffers from the store.
- **Raids**: target score = new sample +10, power +40 (reactor +80), assault (`shared.assault_types`: artillery, artillery wagons, cars, spidertrons, locomotives) +30, turrets −2 each (ammo, electric, fluid; artillery doesn't guard) and refused before stage 2; at most 240 buildings weighed per raid; ruins around the lair (300 tiles) +25 before stage 2, +5 after, minus distance/50. Power in the bite calls everyone at home and keeps the raid up to 3× its duration until it's carried off. A raid goes by train (below) if `plan_ride` finds a stop worth more than the foot target, or half the time anyway; with nobody who can board, on foot.
- **Lifting keeps contents**: when inserters lift a building, `contents.pack` moves what's in it into the cargo; it goes back into the building set down at the lair or at the end, falls out where a carrier dies, and is left on the ground at the lair when a spare copy is taken apart for mass.
- **Ruins** (paranoidal-ruins): buildings of force `neutral` or `AbandonedRuins:enemy`, destructible and minable, with no `pd-legend` entity within 128 tiles (legendary ruins: their structure is `pd-legend`, their neutral pieces indestructible or locked). Decided once per unit number. Setting `capture-ruins`.
- **Artillery** (lair_arms): every artillery turret of the-beast in the lair or infected in place is fired by script, not by the engine (no ammunition, no charting): from stage 4 the base `artillery-projectile` at the player's turrets and radars in range (gun `range`, `min_range`; one search for all artillery within 32 tiles, kept 20 s in `shell_targets`, reset by `hurry("fire")`), every 20 s; from stage 10 `beast-spore-shell` at any infectable building, every 30 s; 1 mass per shell. A spore hit opens a zone (10 tiles, 2 min) and infects up to 3 buildings within 5 tiles.
- **Spidertrons** (lair_arms): infected → occupants out, `vehicle_automatic_targeting_parameters` both on (the old ones kept and put back when it's no longer the-beast's or at the end, with the autopilot cleared), autopilot to the raid or home; at a raid it spawns 3 lone inserters (1 mass each); at home its sample is taken; ammo for mass every 30 s. Not lifted, not cloned; given back with the infected buildings.
- **Track** (lair_rails): needs the sample `straight-rail` analysed. Start: of the player's `straight-rail` nearest the core (radii 200/400/800, 300 each; never its own), the best of the 24 nearest by distance + 150 if its chunk is seen (`LuaForce.is_chunk_visible`, or a connected player within 80) + 300 if a signal or stop is within 12. **Planning**: the junction (best branch off either end) and the piece after it are laid, the junction taken up at once; then 50 pieces per update: `LuaRailEnd.get_rail_extensions(rail planner)` → existing pieces skipped → scored by heading towards the core (an axis required within 72 tiles, or after 400 pieces) → `can_place_entity` (script), trees/rocks/cliffs within 5 tiles destroyed if blocked, next best of 3; at the lair 14 more straight pieces on the same axis (the platform). The plan (`specs`) is kept and the pieces taken up. **Building**: from the platform's end back, one piece every 4 s for 10 MJ, force player, not raised; 3 blocked tries → replan from another rail (its pieces go). **Joining**: the junction last, when its spot is unseen, or 15 min after the rest regardless; told only with `junction-warning`. **Upkeep** (every minute): the player's rail it branched off gone → replan; junction gone → rejoined quietly after 10 min; any other piece gone → laid again.
- **Depot**: a train of its own that gets home (front within 30 of the platform's end or 40 of the core) is taken in: each carriage packed (`contents.pack`, colour, health, owner) and destroyed with `raise_destroy`, and sampled. A carriage creature coming home goes in too once there is a platform (`store_carriage`); one from before is taken in when idle at home (every 30 s). Nothing is left standing on its unsignalled branch, which would hold the player's block at the junction. At the end the depot is set down on the platform as the owner's trains, or spilled as items with its contents.
- **Raids by train**: `plan_ride` needs `rail-raids`, `infect-trains`, the track joined and a locomotive in the depot; of the player's stops (60 random ones more than 100 tiles from its end) the best target within `raid-radius` by `outbreak.score`; 8 best checked with `request_train_path` from the platform out (`all-goals-accessible`) and back to the platform's end from the stop (both directions if it has two locomotives). `board_ride` grows the train on the platform (front locomotive facing out, a second at the back facing in, up to 3 wagons, spaced by `joint_distance + connection_distance`, `snap_to_train_stop = false`), restores contents, infects it (forced), fuels it free (coal, wood, solid fuel or the first fuel by order; later 1 mass per 20 when low), boards the party (`units.board`) and schedules a temporary rail record at the stop. **out** → at `wait_station` or stopped within 14 of the stop: party out beside the carriage nearest the stop as raid members, an alert `ride-arrived` → **waiting** (manual, stopped): anything carrying cargo within 120 and nearer the train than home walks to it and boards → raid over → **boarding** 45 s, the party too → **back** (rail record at the platform's end) → home: everyone out, train taken in. Lost (`no_path`/`no_schedule` 10 s, or 15 min going back) → everyone out, and it's an ordinary infected train for train.lua. Destroyed → everyone out where it last stood. train.lua leaves such a train alone (`lair_rails.keeps`) and inserters never lift it.
- **Lure**: from stage 7 with `lure-station` and an analysed `train-stop` sample, a stop beside one of the platform's last pieces, bound in the direction trains come in, named after the player's stop nearest the core. A train waiting at it is infected and so taken in. At the end it's renamed `[virtual-signal=signal-skull]`.
- **The end** (core destroyed, or no carrier reached the site): creatures set down what they carried and the inserters they were made of; stolen lair buildings go back to their owner (`neutral` for ruins); every infected building is cured and given back; each with a random defect −5…−1 (`ic-defect-5`…`ic-defect-1`) by a fast replace, raised as built. Clones (lair and raid) go to the player with defects too; the depot's carriages are set down on the platform; whoever is aboard its train gets out first, to be set down with the rest; robots and creatures are gone. The defect rule (`shared.random_defect`, user decision 2026-11-03): a defect only if the item has a refining recipe of the quality mod's workshop (mod-data `ic-quality-expansion` `costs[item]`), so the workshop brings it back to normal without any change to that mod. That's every finished product, `no_defect` ones included (belts, turrets, roboports, reactors…); not materials (pipes, rails) nor items with contents or a grid (vehicles, rolling stock, spidertrons). Rolling stock is never replaced.

## Remote interface `the_beast`

`infect(entity)` (on purpose), `is_infected`, `stats`, `spawn(surface, name, position)`, `set_pace`, `arrive()` (false while an outbreak runs), `outbreak()` (status table; `ended` = the key of the end message once it is over), `configure(overrides|nil)` (setting overrides for this save, names without `the-beast-`), `set_tier(n)`, `hurry(what)` with `"landing"`, `"found"`, `"raid"` (true or `"no-target"`/`"no-party"`), `"analyse"`, `"clone"` (free), `"rails"` (the whole track now, free; returns its phase or `"no-sample"`/`"no-core"`), `"fire"` (every artillery fires now; returns how many), `"ride"` (a raid by train now: true, or `"off"`/`"no-track"`/`"busy"`/`"no-train"`/`"no-target"`/`"no-path"`/`"no-party"`), `catalogue()`, `lair_place(name, quality)` (a building set down at the lair with its sample).

## Known hypotheses (not engine-confirmed)

- Electric turrets of the lair fire from a buffer filled by writing `LuaEntity.energy`, with no network.
- A fast replace with only the quality changed keeps contents and connections for every type (checked for chests, artillery turrets and inserters).
- The lure: the player's trains bound for the copied stop name choosing the lair stop (the stop and its name are engine-confirmed; a player train arriving there was not run).
- Trains of the-beast keep to the player's signals as any train does (no signals were in the tests).
- The quiet wait for the junction while its chunk is seen (in the headless tests nothing sees it, so it joined at once).
- Contents with item data (blueprints, armour with grids) coming back intact: only plain items were checked (100 iron plates through the depot and back).
- Spidertron weapons firing by themselves at the player (the targeting parameters are set; no fight was run).

Confirmed by the engine (2026-11-03, `test-runs/beast-smoke6-20261103`): a train of force the-beast finds a path over the player's rails and its own track with a temporary rail record and drives there; `get_rail_extensions("rail")` gives pieces that join the player's line; scripted artillery shells and spore shells land. (`test-runs/beast-smoke7-20261103`): the track planned and laid from its end with the junction last; a stolen train going home and taken into the depot with its cargo; `request_train_path` with rail-end starts and train-stop goals; a train grown from the depot on the platform, double-headed, connected, fuelled, reaching a player's stop by a temporary rail record and reporting it; boarding, a lifted accumulator carried home aboard; the lure stop bound and named; the depot set down at the end with its cargo.
- Lair buildings are not raised as built while the outbreak holds them; mods that only track built events learn of them when they are given back.
