---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-03
  Bugfixes:
    - Generator creatures read the animation inside each direction of the Factorio 2.1 picture set; passing its wrapper as a sprite caused a missing-width error on startup.
  Major Features:
    - The protomolecule replaces the biomechanical virus. Mod name Paranoidal-Protomolecule-Beast; prototype, setting and remote interface names stay as they were.
    - The outbreak: it lands once per map, weak, on a far outpost, takes what it can there without a fight for a few minutes, and withdraws to a lair 150-300 tiles further out, where the first creature to get there founds its core. After 30-60 minutes of build-up it raids the base: a party walks to the edge of the base nearest the lair, infects its target and a little around it, and goes home with whatever is lifted. Infection only spreads inside the landing site and a raid's target while they last; what's left infected elsewhere lies dormant until a zone covers it again.
    - The lair: what's carried home is unpacked beside the core as its own building (rolling stock stays a creature); beyond two of the same building, further ones are taken apart for mass, except power buildings. Each new kind of building becomes a sample, analysed one at a time, faster with stolen machines. The core heals everything of its own around it.
    - Power first: steam engines and turbines, boilers and heat exchangers, burner generators, reactors, accumulators, solar panels and wind turbines can be infected and lifted from the start, as new creatures (working names Dynamo walker, Stoker, Heart, Cell, Mirror, Vane), and are what gathering inserters and raids go for first. A raid that infects power calls everyone at home and stays, up to three times as long, until it's carried off.
    - The lair's power: the core's own 1 MW plus the rated power of every stolen power building set down there, without fuel. Research is one stage per 30 minutes on the core's own power, faster with stolen power (twice as fast at 5 MW more, at most 6 times). An energy store (600 MJ, plus a hundred times each stolen accumulator's buffer) pays for clones and its electric turrets.
    - Cloning: from analysed samples, for mass (from the core, stolen drills in the lair and spare copies taken apart) and energy, turrets around the core first; up to 400 lair buildings, of them at most 60 turrets and 8 artillery turrets. Turrets cloned beside a raid on the base cover it and break down when it goes. Its turrets get ammunition and power from the lair.
    - What it attacks with: artillery turrets and wagons, cars, tanks, spidertrons and trains can be taken from the start and are what it goes for after power. An artillery turret is lifted as a Howitzer, a car or tank as a Crawler that turns like a train (working names); an artillery wagon is a wagon. Whoever is in an infected vehicle is thrown out. Lifted vehicles stay creatures at the lair, to raid with; lifted carriages go into its depot once it has a platform, and stay creatures until then.
    - Its artillery, in the lair or infected where it stands, is fired by script: from research stage 4 the base game's shell at the player's turrets and radars in range, from the last stage spore shells at any building, which open a zone where they land and infect the nearest few buildings. A unit of mass per shell.
    - Spidertrons it takes target by themselves, walk home and to every raid by autopilot, set down a few walking inserters at a raid and get ammunition from the lair.
    - Its own railway: once it has a sample of rail (rails in any zone it opens) and has analysed it, it works out a track from the player's rail nearest the lair, preferring one that nobody sees and away from signals and stops, to a straight platform by the lair, a batch of pieces a second. It lays it from its own end, one piece every 4 seconds for 10 MJ, clearing trees, rocks and cliffs, and the piece that joins the player's line goes in last, when nobody sees that spot or after 15 minutes. The player isn't told unless a setting says so. The track is the player's; whatever of it is taken up it lays again, the junction quietly after 10 minutes, and if the rail it branched off goes it starts over elsewhere.
    - Its depot: a train it infects goes home along the track if it finds the way (otherwise it's driven and lifted as before), and at home it is taken into the lair with what's in it, so that nothing stands on its branch holding the player's line at the junction. Its carriages become samples.
    - Raids by train: with the track joined and a locomotive in its depot, a raid may go by train, to whichever of the player's stops it can get to and back from has the most it wants around it. It grows the train on its platform from what it took (a locomotive at each end if it has two, up to three wagons), fuels it and puts the raid party aboard. At the stop the party gets out and raids (the player gets an alert), whatever they lift is carried aboard, and when the raid is over the party gets back on and it goes home, where it's taken in again and they unload. If it loses its way it runs on as an infected train; if it's destroyed whoever's aboard gets out where it stood.
    - The lure: from research stage 7, with a sample of a train stop (from a zone with one), it puts a stop at the end of its platform under the name of the player's stop nearest the lair. Every train that stops there is infected and taken in.
    - What's inside a building it lifts (its inventories, equipment grid and fluids) is kept: it's put back when the building is set down in the lair or at the end, it falls out where a carrier is killed, and it's left on the ground in the lair when a spare copy is taken apart. The same goes for the trains in its depot.
    - The lair's sums (power, store, drills, machines, turrets, copies) are kept up as buildings come and go instead of being counted every second, and its turrets are fed a batch at a time, so a lair of hundreds costs little. Its buildings in the lair stand idle with the status "Assimilated", all but its gun and laser turrets: a stolen reactor with fuel in it doesn't heat up, furnaces don't smelt, radars don't chart. Its artillery shares one search for targets.
    - Ruins: buildings of paranoidal-ruins that nobody has claimed, and what's left of partly taken apart ones, can be raided and infected; ruins near the lair are what it goes for before it will fight. Legendary ruins are never touched.
    - The end: when the core is destroyed (or no carrier reaches the site), whatever creatures carried and the inserters they were made of are set down where they stand, stolen lair buildings go back to who they were taken from, its clones (in the lair and beside a raid) go to the player, the trains in its depot are set down on its platform with what was in them, its stop stops luring, and infected buildings are given back, each with a random defect from -5 to -1 if the refining workshop can bring its item back to normal (every finished product but materials and items with contents or a grid, such as vehicles and rolling stock); creatures, spore zones and robots are gone.
    - Research: 10 stages with a message for each. Assembling machines and furnaces, then turrets, fluids and roboports can be taken one stage at a time; it stops keeping out of fights; artillery fire (stage 4); the lure station (stage 7); armour up to 45%, faster healing, larger raids; from stage 8 infected poles pass it along their copper wires; spore shells (stage 10). Creature speed rises from 25% to 150% of their own pace.
    - Until it stops keeping out of fights, its creatures don't attack, don't drill and go home when badly hurt.
  Changes:
    - Paranoidal fork for Factorio 2.0 (2.0.77): factorio_version 2.0, base >= 2.0.0, space-age as an ordered optional dependency.
    - The other end of an underground belt and what pipes are connected to are read from LuaEntity.neighbours, as underground_belt_neighbour and fluidbox_neighbours are 2.1 only.
    - Only the player force's buildings are infected, and only those placed from an item and selectable: hidden parts of other mods' buildings are skipped, not removed. Factorissimo factory buildings and their parts are never infected. Decided once per entity name.
    - Belts and pipes are the only things whose force is switched while looking for what they connect to.
    - The legs are drawn from the vanilla 2.0.77 inserter sprites kept in prototypes/vanilla-inserters.lua instead of data.raw, so the mod loads whatever other mods did to the inserters before it. Furnace fires and the lamp light are swapped for the purple ones only when they are the exact vanilla files, and other ones are tinted. A building without a picture this can read gets no creature, which is logged.
    - The march along the railway keeps the rail and its end direction instead of a LuaRailEnd.
    - on_entity_died is filtered to infection markers and spider units.
    - Remote "arrive" returns whether an outpost was found.
    - Creatures are made in data-final-fixes, after the reskins, Paranoidal lighting, HeroTurretRedux and zzzparanoidal (optional dependencies, for load order only), so they look like the buildings as they end up.
    - What each building becomes is passed to the control stage as mod-data (the-beast-compounds) instead of a fixed list. Inserters gather only at infected buildings, looked up by type and the Beast's force instead of a list of names.
    - Buildings that other mods' scripts look after are never infected: legendary ruins' relics, the BnL distress beacon, the quality control post, Nanobots' roboport interface, Bio Industries' arboretum area and Rampant's buildings.
    - Lights and smoke are dropped from a creature's body by the file's own name as plain text. The old pattern matched "light" anywhere in the path and dropped every layer of a mod with "light" in its name.
    - An infected train that nothing can lift stops once it has got somewhere, instead of running on for good.
    - A creature that climbs onto walls keeps what it carries. A spidertron given back targets as it did before and stops walking off. Spidertrons set down their inserters at a raid without loading a file at runtime.
    - Creature icons are taken as icon or icons.
  Features:
    - Other mods' buildings become creatures too: drills and pumpjacks (strider), machines (harvester), furnaces (spewer), chests including logistic ones (tripod), radars, lamps, turrets, poles, locomotives and wagons, by type. Up to 5 tiles across; at 4-5 tiles they take two more inserters, and have more health to match. Rolling stock of any size. One set of legs (the plain inserter's, or the long-handed one's for poles) and its walled version per building, so the build adds about 800 creature prototypes. A building whose picture can't be read is logged and stays an infected building.
    - HeroTurretRedux's ranked turrets are lifted as their base turret (the rank is lost). A lit pole breaks and is lifted as its lamp; the pole counts as a sample too.
    - Pumpjacks keep their frame under the moving head when lifted.
    - Startup settings: creature health and damage multipliers, infection health.
    - Map settings: power buildings can be infected; taking ruins; clones in the lair, turrets cloned beside a raid, lair buildings at most (400). Research stage time is now at the core's own power, 30 minutes by default. Artillery can be infected; cars, tanks and spidertrons can be infected; its artillery fires shells; its artillery fires spore shells; it lays its own track; its station lures trains; raids by train; a warning when its track joins the player's line (off by default).
    - Map settings: automatic arrival, first arrival delay counted from when the mod was added, required technology, minimum outpost distance, drills and radius, drills infected on landing, spread speed, which kinds of buildings can be infected (walls, pipes, poles and lamps, turrets, roboports, trains), inserters coming alive, inserters lifting machines, climbing walls, with the original defaults. New: the enemy evolution it lands at, picked at random between two settings (80-90% by default); landing on the side of the base away from the Robot Army replicators' citadel, lair included (on by default); landing time, lair distance, build-up time, research stage time, raid interval, duration and radius, creature speed at the start and the end. No repeat landings.
    - Remote interface: outbreak (status, with power, energy, mass, clones, artillery, spidertrons and the track), configure (setting overrides for one save), set_tier, hurry (landing, found, raid, analyse, clone, rails, ride, fire), catalogue, lair_place (a building set down at the lair with its sample); infect is on purpose, so it works outside the zones. /beast-arrive does nothing while an outbreak is going on.
  Locale:
    - Russian locale. Mod settings and the unknown technology message in English.
    - Clinical texts for the landing, research stages and the end of the outbreak; new mod title and description; names of the power, artillery and vehicle creatures; the rail junction message; the alert for a train at a raid.
  Info:
    - AGENT-NOTES.md: how the mod works, its storage, remote interface and build links, and what to change for Factorio 2.1 (every place marked "-- 2.1:" in the code).
  Compatibility:
    - Ported to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version 2.1, base >= 2.1.0; underground belts and piping spread through underground_belt_neighbour and fluidbox_neighbours (LuaEntity.neighbours is gone); generator creatures read the 2.1 pictures; a pumpjack creature takes its frame from the always-drawn working visualisation drawn below the animation (MiningDrillPrototype.base_picture is gone).
