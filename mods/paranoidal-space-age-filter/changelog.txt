Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Major Features:
    - First version (Space Age branch of Paranoidal, user decision 2026-11-07, variant A1). With Space Age enabled, mods["space-age"] reads nil in the settings and data stages of every other mod, so their own Space Age branches stay off; Factorio's own mods (base, core, quality, recycler, elevated-rails, space-age) still see it. The first time a mod asks, the log names the mod and the line. Without Space Age the mod does nothing.
    - No dependencies: the mod loads right after base, before every mod that has a dependency, so no mod reads the table before it is hidden.
    - With Space Age enabled, its changes to the base game are undone right after its data.lua (space-age-platforms.lua): every technology, recipe and item is put back as it was right before it, in the same table, so the mods loaded after it build on the same base game as without the expansion. Kept: the technology space-science-pack as Space Age made it (it unlocks the space science recipe made on a platform), the satellite stays removed, the rocket silo technology keeps unlocking platforms, the starter pack and the foundation, the rocket silo launches to platforms, the item flags of the rocket silo. Its changes to buildings (where they may be built, heating, frozen graphics) stay: they matter only on platforms and planets. The rocket silo needs 100 rocket parts again (Space Age: 50).
    - A prototype of another mod that Space Age defines again under the same name keeps the mod's version (Bob's carbon, tungsten ore, plate and carbide, lithium plate).
    - Space Age technologies are hidden except the space platform, its thrusters and the landing pad unloading bay; its worker robot speed 7 is removed, as base worker robot speed 6 is infinite again. The space platform technology also unlocks travel to space platforms by rocket (in Space Age only the hidden planet discoveries do it). Its recipes that no visible technology unlocks are hidden, and so are recipes of any mod that use a Space Age fluid nothing makes (Bob's fluoroketone barrels).
    - The Space Age planets keep no resources in their map settings (Angels then removes stone and tungsten ore as without Space Age, RSO places the same ores at home), its own resources have no autoplace, its planets, space locations and routes are hidden.
  Info:
    - Control scripts read script.active_mods, which another mod cannot change; their Space Age checks also test for this mod where needed (Big-Monsters, RampantFixed, the bobores 2.1.0 migration).
    - The load order is the one without Space Age: in the Space Age branch the 22 mods of the build that had an optional dependency on space-age no longer have it (see their changelogs); this mod itself cannot change the order.
