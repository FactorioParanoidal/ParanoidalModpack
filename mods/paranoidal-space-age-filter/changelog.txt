---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Major Features:
    - First version (Space Age branch of Paranoidal, user decision 2026-11-07, variant A1). With Space Age enabled, mods["space-age"] reads nil in the settings and data stages of every other mod, so their own Space Age branches stay off; Factorio's own mods (base, core, quality, recycler, elevated-rails, space-age) still see it. The first time a mod asks, the log names the mod and the line. Without Space Age the mod does nothing.
    - No dependencies: the mod loads right after base, before every mod that has a dependency, so no mod reads the table before it is hidden.
    - With Space Age enabled, its changes to the base game are undone right after its data.lua (space-age-platforms.lua): every technology, recipe and item is put back as it was right before it, in the same table, so the mods loaded after it build on the same base game as without the expansion. Kept: the technology space-science-pack as Space Age made it (it unlocks the space science recipe made on a platform), the satellite stays removed, the rocket silo technology keeps unlocking platforms, the starter pack and the foundation, the rocket silo launches to platforms, the item flags of the rocket silo. Its changes to buildings (where they may be built, heating, frozen graphics) stay: they matter only on platforms and planets. The rocket silo needs 100 rocket parts again (Space Age: 50).
    - A prototype of another mod that Space Age defines again under the same name keeps the mod's version (Bob's carbon, tungsten ore, plate and carbide, lithium plate).
    - Space Age technologies are hidden except the space platform, its thrusters and the landing pad unloading bay; its worker robot speed 7 is removed, as base worker robot speed 6 is infinite again. The space platform technology also unlocks travel to space platforms by rocket (in Space Age only the hidden planet discoveries do it). Its recipes that no visible technology unlocks are hidden, and so are recipes of any mod that use a Space Age fluid nothing makes (Bob's fluoroketone barrels).
    - The Space Age planets keep only tiles in their map settings: no resources (Angels then removes stone and tungsten ore as without Space Age, RSO places the same ores at home), no trees, rocks or decoratives (a mod that does not see Space Age may take the autoplace off its plants, as Alien Biomes did, and a planet listing them does not load); again in data-final-fixes for what later mods add. Its own resources have no autoplace, its planets, space locations and routes are hidden.
    - With Space Age enabled, rocket payloads that return items to the ground (Expanded Rocket Payloads shuttles, satellites, the space lab, the orbital solar collector) weigh 500 kg: a rocket going to platforms takes any items up to 1 t, so at their own 1 kg ten of them fitted into one rocket (without Space Age the rocket holds one item); now two fit.
    - paranoidal-warp (own warp content of the Space Age branch) saw Space Age too; the mod was removed from the branch on 2026-11-08 (user decision: Space Age planets after the rocket instead of the warp), so the list of mods that see Space Age is Factorio's own again.
    - 2026-11-08 (user decision: classic Paranoidal up to the rocket, the Space Age planets after it): the planets, their places, routes and technologies are no longer hidden and keep their resources, trees and rocks; victory is reaching the solar system edge. Space Age technologies unlock only Space Age recipes (Bob's turbo belts and battery mk3 equipment, the heat pipe, heat exchanger and steam turbine stay with their home technologies; the emptied turbo belt technology is hidden) and all come after the rocket silo (the Fulgora chain after the Fulgora discovery; electric weapons damage 1 and steel plate productivity after space science; artillery shell damage 1 after metallurgic science; captive biter spawner without the hidden kovarex enrichment). Home technologies do not unlock recipes with Space Age materials (Bob's fluoroketone barrels go to the cryogenic plant).
    - Every planet has its own materials under new names, so the home ones keep their recipes and the planets cannot be skipped: wolframite (ore and deposit), refractory beam and pobedit of Vulcanus (Bob's tungsten ore, plate and carbide), ice silver and cryogas of Aquilo (Bob's lithium plate and ammonia), ancient debris and its salvage of Fulgora (the BnL start scrap; the salvage comes with the Fulgora discovery), stone outcrops of Gleba (stone itself stays). Space Age's carbon from coal becomes a recipe of its own for the home carbon. Every reference of Space Age's prototypes follows; the planet controls named after home resources get the new names (with underscores, as noise expressions need), so Angels removes Bob's tungsten ore and the home stone deposit as without Space Age and RSO places the same ores at home.
    - RSO Reforged leaves the Space Age planets and their own resources to the game's generator (its hook data.rso_ignore_planets / data.rso_ignore_resource_entities).
    - A fluid that comes from a tile or a resource (lava, the oceans, brine, vents) counts as made: the recipes using it stay.
    - Recipes of Space Age (zzzparanoidal: icon matching) are told by prototype, not by name: a home recipe or technology of the same name defined after Space Age stays a home one.
    - Mods of other planets (2026-11-08, user decision: Secretas & Frozeta, then Moshine, if nothing changes before the rocket): secretas sees Space Age; its prototypes count as Space Age's own content after the rocket: references to the renamed planet materials follow (Secretas uses the tungsten plate of Vulcanus, the lithium plate of Aquilo and the scrap of Fulgora: refractory beam, ice silver, ancient debris), RSO Reforged leaves Frozeta and the spaceship scrap to the game's generator, its technologies unlock only its own and Space Age recipes and come after the rocket silo, zzzparanoidal prices and icons treat its recipes as planet recipes.
  Info:
    - Control scripts read script.active_mods, which another mod cannot change; their Space Age checks also test for this mod where needed (Big-Monsters, RampantFixed, the bobores 2.1.0 migration).
    - The load order is the one without Space Age: in the Space Age branch the 22 mods of the build that had an optional dependency on space-age no longer have it (see their changelogs); this mod itself cannot change the order.
