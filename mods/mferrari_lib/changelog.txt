Version: 0.6.6
  Graphics:
    - thumbnail.png is redrawn at 144x144 after the old smaller picture: text and simple shapes are drawn again, the rest is resampled and sharpened.
  Changes:
    - Paranoidal fork: enemies that come in levels with one picture show the level on the icon (upper right, 1-9 and A = 10, as on the RampantFixed units), so the list of turret priority targets tells them apart: fake humans (the boss kinds also get a skull in the lower left corner), boss biters and boss spitters 1-10, flying saucers 1-10. The flying saucers also show the level in the name. Helper prototypes/level-icon.lua, also used by Big Monsters.
    - Paranoidal fork: CallFrenzyAttack hands the attack to RampantFixed (remote strikePosition_ExtCtrl) when it manages the surface: Rampant squads from the nests nearest to the target instead of up to 450 single units commanded one by one; no attack while the Rampant AI is peaceful. The old command stays without RampantFixed
    - Paranoidal fork: a dying reactor no longer creates a "nuke-explosion" (sight and sound without damage); real meltdowns come from the reactor prototypes
  Bugfixes:
    - Paranoidal fork: CallFrenzyAttack no longer reads an invalid target entity
    - Paranoidal fork: get_pos_near_from_aproach returns the point at the given distance on the straight line to the approaching position (used pos.x for y and offset both axes, a diagonal 1.41 times longer, out of the boss spidertron and flying saucer weapon range)
    - Fixed the missing-science-pack log message (it used an undefined variable).
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: science pack checks accept every item type (2.1 vanilla science packs are plain items, mod packs may stay tools).
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: loot entries use the 2.1 format (type, name, amount_min, amount_max, independent_probability instead of item, count_min, count_max, probability).
    - Factorio 2.1 (Paranoidal decision D14 A): build_base_evolution_requirement was removed with its runtime read and the build-base-evolution-requirement prototype filter, so CallWormAttack (Big-Monsters) would fail; the evolution values of the worms are kept in mod-data mferrari-worm-evolution (data-final-fixes.lua, 2.0 default 0) and the worm choice reads them; the dead field is removed from every prototype.
    - Factorio 2.1 runtime API: fluid transfer uses extract_fluid (remove_fluid{} was renamed); frozen and indestructible entities use disabled_by_script and minable_flag (LuaEntity::active and minable became read-only).
