Version: 9.1.0
Date: 2026-10-24
  Features:
    - Legendary ruins: 22 huge sites (64x64 to 160x160 tiles, built over several chunks) no closer than
      100 chunks (setting) beyond the edge of the starting zone; each appears once per map. Trials (beaten
      with early gear): Labyrinth of the Minotaur, Sapper's Field, Pharaoh's Pyramid, "Searchlight" Vault,
      Dune, Broken Gates, Archivist's Crypt, Hall of Mirrors, Frozen Moment, Forerunners' Bazaar, Forge of
      Hephaestus. Citadels (green-science weapons): Colosseum, Hive of the Queen, "Red Director" Works,
      Reactor "Half-Life", Inferno Oilfield, Plague Quarantine, Citadel of the Hammer, Mothership,
      Observatory on the Cliff, Yggdrasil, Cemetery of the First Colonists.
    - Sanctuary rules while a legendary ruin is not beaten: untouchable walls (full-tile collision), no
      building, no tiles, flying vehicles are thrown out, the treasury is sealed and empty. The floor of
      a legendary ruin cannot be taken up (tiles laid by players after the victory can).
    - Rewards: 22 relic buildings without recipes (not recycled) and stacks of quality 4-7 (trials) or
      6-10 (citadels). Lost forever if the ruin is ruined.
    - Map settings: legendary ruins on/off, ferocity (legendary / harsh / truce, independent of the
      core's hostile defences), chance per chunk, distance, spacing, repeat after all.
    - Discovery with lore and hints, lock/unlock/skull map tags, /ruins counts legendary ruins,
      /legendary-spawn <name> for admins, remote functions legendary_reserved and spawn_legendary.
    - Facts about the legendary ruins for achievement mods: one custom event (remote
      get_on_legendary_event) raised when a ruin is found, beaten or ruined, for special feats, relics
      and sanctuary events. Ruins laid out by /legendary-spawn or the remote interface report only that.
      The achievements themselves live in MxlChievements-Reforged.
    - RSO old mines are not placed inside legendary ruins. A legendary ruin is given up (and may appear
      elsewhere) when players already have buildings in its area; corpses of characters are kept.
  Changes:
    - Version number starts with 9 (Paranoidal mod).
    - New ruins with 30-100 landfill for the small holes under wide blueprints early in the game:
      Landfill cache (small), Landfill yard (medium), Abandoned earthworks (large).

Version: 1.0.1
Date: 2026-10-14
  Features:
    - Remote function "spawn_mine" for RSO Reforged: places an old mine ruin of this set (geologist's box,
      miners' village, prospecting drill, mining camp) next to a rich ore field; the journal records it.
    - Remote function "signal_ruin" for Ore Radar: marks a random undiscovered ruin with a "?" map
      tag for the force. The tag disappears when a player discovers the ruin.
  Bugfixes:
    - Clearing a surface drops its undiscovered ruins from the journal.
  Changes:
    - Stonehenge: the inner ring is made of huge rocks (stone and coal) instead of RampantFixed loot stones;
      the combat power-up capsule they held was removed from RampantFixed.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-11
  Features:
    - Ruin set "paranoidal": 170 new ruins (87 small, 57 medium, 26 large) plus all ruins of the
      "base" set when AbandonedRuins-base is enabled. It is the default and the only ruin set
      offered besides "none"; a stored "base" value falls back to "paranoidal".
    - 1.1 rails of "base" ruins (curved-rail, diagonal straight-rail) are rebuilt with 2.0 legacy
      rails, so railSection3, railSection4 and eFurnaceRail spawn completely.
    - Defect-quality junk and normal-quality starter kits for the Paranoidal defect system.
    - Dangerous ruins (turrets, worms, mines, flamethrowers). Their distance limits apply only
      when the core setting "Disable distance dependence of ruins" is turned off.
    - Discovery journal: first-time finds are announced, map markers, /ruins shows progress.
    - Oil traps "pd-flo-trap-pumping-station" and "pd-flo-trap-tank-farm" for Flammable Oils: a pipeline
      starts burning when a player comes close and the fire reaches the storage tanks.
