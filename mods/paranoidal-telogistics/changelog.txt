Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-10
  Major Features:
    - First version (Space Age branch of Paranoidal only, user request 2026-11-10): cargo teleporters rewritten from scratch after Telogistics 1.2.0 by S6X (GPLv3; its teleporter graphics and sounds are reused, the code is new).
    - Three tiers: cargo teleporter (48 slots, recharge 20 s at 750 MW, five times the original's power), quantum cargo teleporter (80 slots, 10 s, 1500 MW) and singularity cargo teleporter (120 slots, 5 s, 3000 MW); one send is always 15 GJ. Each tier is crafted from the previous one and goes to the next by the upgrade planner.
    - Each tier belongs to a planet (user 2026-11-11; the first test version counted visited planets 3/4/5 — removed): tier 1 is researched on Muluna after the interstellar science pack (1000 units, home packs + space + interstellar), tier 2 after the metallurgic science pack of Vulcanus (1500, + metallurgic), tier 3 after the electromagnetic science pack of Fulgora (2000, + metallurgic and electromagnetic). Each tier needs the previous one.
    - Paranoidal prices from the planet of the tier: tier 1 100 lunite, 50 microcellular plastic, 20 data cables, 2 satellite flight computers, 10 large accumulators 3; tier 2 the previous tier, 100 refractory beams, 100 pobedit, 30 low density structures; tier 3 the previous tier, 200 superconductors, 100 supercapacitors, 100 holmium plates. Rocket control units are not used: zzzparanoidal creates them only in its data-final-fixes, and RITEG drops recipe ingredients that do not exist before that.
  Features:
    - The teleporter is a chest: slot filters, the red bar and circuit reading of its contents work as in a vanilla chest. A hidden charger under it draws the tier's power from the electric network until it holds one send (shown in the power statistics as the teleporter; a weak network charges longer); switched off teleporters draw nothing.
    - Receivers draw power all the time (user 2026-11-11): 50, 100 and 200 MW by tier, a native consumer of the electric network. A receiver without power (buffer below half: no network or a network short of power) takes no cargo and shows "No power"; a sender fills only the powered receivers of its channel and waits with "Receivers have no power" when none has power, keeping cargo and charge. The panel shows how many receivers are powered and the receive power.
    - Settings panel next to the chest window: mode (send, receive, off), channel (any signal; empty is the common channel), destination planet, when to send (as soon as charged, when full, on a circuit condition over the red and green wire), live status and charge, receivers and free slots on the destination, items sent and received.
    - A sender moves its contents stack by stack into the receivers of its channel on the destination planet, starting after the receiver served last; quality, spoilage, health, ammo, equipment grids, blueprints and item inventories move unchanged (transfer of the stack itself). Receiver slot filters and the red bar are respected. Nothing moved — no energy spent.
    - Flash and sounds at both ends of a send; the status line of the entity shows what the teleporter waits for.
    - Settings survive copy-paste, blueprints, ghosts of destroyed teleporters, fast replace and the upgrade planner (with the charge); a teleporter removed by another mod without an event takes its charger along.
    - Not buildable on space platforms (gravity) or inside Factorissimo buildings (ceiling).
    - Graphics recompressed (pingo 1.29): building sprite, icon and thumbnail -s4 -quality=75 (worst 36.4 dB), technology icon and shadow -quality=45 (36.0 dB / identical), the additive glow animation lossless; 3.31 MB -> 2.63 MB.
