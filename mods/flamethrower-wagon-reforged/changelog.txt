Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-10-27
  Major Features:
    - Paranoidal fork of Flamethrower Wagon 2.1.0 (fgardt) for Factorio 2.0, renamed to flamethrower-wagon-reforged. Prototype names are unchanged; the original mod is marked incompatible.
  Optimizations:
    - The control script is rewritten. Without flamethrower wagons only name-filtered build and clone events and on_train_created stay registered; on_tick, on_object_destroyed, on_train_changed_state, on_entity_died and on_player_rotated_entity are registered only while wagons exist (the same rule on load).
    - Wagons of trains waiting at a station or signal, or without a path or schedule, are not polled. A train state change wakes them up. A stopped manual train is polled for its speed only.
    - Moving wagons are teleported from reused position tables without new allocations; a stopped manual train costs two reads per tick (validity and speed).
    - Fuel service no longer runs for all wagons at once every 30 ticks: each wagon is serviced once per 60 ticks, spread evenly over the ticks. Turrets that are full are skipped without reading the wagon tank.
  Bugfixes:
    - Uses the Factorio 2.0 fluid API (fluidbox, get_fluid, remove_fluid); the 2.1 calls get_fluid_capacity, add_fluid, clear_fluid and add_fluid_box_linked_connection do not exist in 2.0.
    - The turrets get the fuels of the build's flamethrower turret at data-final-fixes (Bob's and Angels change that list after this mod copies the turret). Only these fuels are moved from the wagon tank, other fluids stay in it.
    - The turrets are no longer emptied when the wagon tank runs dry; the fuel left in them is still fired. A turret holding another fuel fires it first and switches to the wagon fuel when nearly empty; a fluid that is no fuel is replaced at once.
    - The fluid temperature is kept when moving fuel into the turrets.
    - Cloning a wagon no longer destroys the source wagon and no longer crashes for an unregistered source. Turrets copied by an area clone are removed; the clone gets its own turrets with the fuel and health of the source turrets.
    - Wagons created by scripts without raise_built get their turrets from on_train_created; on configuration change unknown wagons are adopted and orphan turrets are removed.
    - On curves the turret base direction follows the wagon quarter (north/east/south/west), so the gun picture stays on the roof after a turn; before, the turrets kept the direction they were built with.
    - When a wagon is flipped (rotation) the turrets swap roles, so the turret at the front keeps the matching base direction.
    - A turret that is killed now kills its wagon (death events, remnants, ghost) instead of silently deleting it.
    - Turrets settle exactly at the stopping point of the wagon.
    - The fire resistance of the wagon is found by type instead of assuming it is the first entry.
  Balancing:
    - Recipe for the Paranoidal build: fluid wagon, 2 flamethrower turrets, pump, 8 steel bearings, 20 steel plates, 5 s (was fluid wagon, 2 flamethrower turrets, 6 engine units, 10 steel plates, 1.5 s). The turrets already contain 10 engine units.
    - Technology right after Flamethrower and Fluid wagon, on the flamethrower science tier: 100 × red, green and military science, 30 s (was 350 × red, green, blue and military science, also after Chemical science pack).
    - Stack size 10, as the other fluid wagons of the build (was 5).
  Changes:
    - Item and entity icons no longer inherit icons or item pictures of a reskinned fluid wagon. Recipe and technology are defined directly instead of being copied from the fluid wagon recipe and the flamethrower technology.
    - Russian locale added; mod name and description locale keys fixed.
    - Remote interface flamethrower-wagon-reforged.stats for diagnostics: number of wagons, number of polled wagons and their unit numbers, whether the dynamic handlers are on.
