---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 9.0.0
Date: 2026-11-02
  Major Features:
    - New mod of the build: a fork of Dynamic Rain 1.2.0 (valertron, MIT) for Factorio 2.0 with parts of Real Rain 1.2.15 (Squishy1870): storm stages drizzle - rain - heavy - storm - monsoon, three drop layers with wind buckets and gusts, splashes and water ripples, puddles after the rain, rolling thunder and its Mixkit sounds.
    - Lightning strikes on 2.0 without Space Age: the engine lightning needs the Space Age feature flag, so a strike is scripted (target priorities of Dynamic Rain, effects on poles, radars, robots, creatures, trees, fish and flammable things, a discharge chain) and drawn with the Space Age impact flash, ground streamers and sounds copied into the mod. The engine variant of Dynamic Rain is kept commented out for 2.1.
    - Rain slowly puts ordinary fires out in waves from the edges with steam (a big fire within 30 s) and washes enemy acid away; lightning fires and burning flammable fluids stay.
    - In the rain enemy fire and acid deal no damage and fire damage of flamethrowers, flamethrower turrets and wagons is halved (settings).
    - Rain slows paranoidal-flammable-fluids hazards down 2.5 times (remote hazard_slowdown): burning tanks, fire through pipes, ammunition cook-off. Lightning sets storage tanks and ammunition stores off through that mod.
    - Weather forecast: the technology "prain-weather-forecast" (after bob-radar-2, 50 red and 50 green packs). At dawn of the first and the middle day of every NightBrightness season (every third day without seasons) the force gets one chat line about the days until the next forecast: clear weather, rain or storm, with "unlikely", "likely" or "certain" by the reliability of its working radars (1 point 10 %, 10 points 50 %, 100 points 100 %; bob radars count by tier). With the rest of the chance the forecast lies: it promises what certainly will not happen.
    - Rains are planned 4 game days ahead and follow the NightBrightness seasons (setting prain-seasons): no rain in winter, one try with 10 % per summer, spring and fall as usual, a fall rain ends before the winter. The forecast only reads the plan; commands and the remote start_phase override it.
    - Darker sky in the rain (always, by stage, up to 28 % in a monsoon) and a short white screen flash from a close strike or thunder (per-player setting prain-screen-flash).
    - Enemies are bolder in the rain: bite and spit damage +15 % by day and +25 % at night (ammo damage modifiers of the enemy force, added and taken back as a difference) and attacks more often (enemy_attack_pollution_consumption_modifier divided by the same factor while it rains).
    - Lightning in a gun or artillery turret starts the cook-off of its ammunition like in a chest (remote cook_off of paranoidal-flammable-fluids; turrets became lightning targets); a storage tank is set burning by remote ignite_tank with the cause "lightning".
    - Connection point for lightning attractors of other mods: mod-data "paranoidal-rain-attractors" (range, priority, mode attract/absorb, self_damage, energy_share for the planned rod tiers) and the event on_lightning_strike (remote get_events).
    - Splashes by ground: water ripples, light splashes on paved tiles, small dark splashes on soil; the ground is read from a 10x6 grid of the view refreshed every 1.5 s, not per drop.
  Graphics:
    - New thumbnail "RAIN · PARANOIDAL" and the icon of the weather forecast technology, made from the base game radar and drawn storm clouds.
  Changes:
    - Peaceful map: on a map whose Nauvis was generated without enemy bases (enemy-base size "none", e.g. the preset "Paranoidal: no enemies") lightning is only the flash and the thunder: no damage, kills, fire, brownout, radar outage, drained buffers, discharge chain, cook-off or lightning rod charge.
    - Settings renamed with the prefix "prain-"; solar power in the rain 75 % by default; weather only on Nauvis by default; no rain during the first 30-60 minutes of a map.
    - Removed: the weather statistics window and its hidden force (a desync risk on save load in multiplayer, and force switching of players), the debug overlay, Jetpack and Space Age planet support, the test hooks. Counters are shown by /prain status.
    - A radar struck by lightning is disabled by script for 20 s instead of being moved to the neutral force.
  Optimizations:
    - A shared drop limit per tick (prain-max-drops-per-tick, 300): far zoom and many players thin the rain instead of slowing the game.
    - Only own settings and surface events rebuild the state; chunk counts are not recounted for every new surface.
    - The fire/acid damage handler is registered only while it rains.
  Compatibility:
    - Paranoidal port to Factorio 2.1: factorio_version set to 2.1.
